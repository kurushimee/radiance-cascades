## Add bounces

Start with [Bounces](../topics/core_theory.md#bounces) for how cascades feed back into themselves and [Bounces](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#bounces) for the single-frame budget. Other solvers' multi-bounce schemes are in [Multi-bounce](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#multi-bounce).

- Get more than one bounce of GI in one frame: Stored hit distances or a coarse second solve; trade-off: no 3D route measured. ([Bounces](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#bounces), single_frame_3d_rc)
- Add a second bounce to a single frame: A coarse second solve two LODs up, with 1/16 of the first's probes and rays on probes that follow surfaces; trade-off: 4× the spacing, for a bounce that carries a third of two bounces' light at albedo 0.5. ([Where the evidence stops](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#where-the-evidence-stops), single_frame_3d_rc)
- Get multi-bounce GI from one RC solve: Jacobi iteration over frames; one bounce of lag per frame. ([Bounces](../topics/core_theory.md#bounces), core_theory)
- Cost a single-frame multi-bounce pass: A bounce by repeated RC iteration costs a full solve, with stored hit distances a re-light and a merge; in a closed scene `n` bounces hold `1 − ρ^n` of the indirect light. ([Multi-bounce](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#multi-bounce), light_transport_alternatives)
- Get multi-bounce light within one frame: Re-solve per bounce in one frame; trade-off: one full solve per bounce. ([Single-frame assessment](../topics/holographic_rc.md#single-frame-assessment), holographic_rc)
- Bounce light without a second solve: Last frame's fluence feeds the next frame's seed; trade-off: one frame of latency. ([cute_framework and folkjs](../topics/holographic_rc_implementations.md#cute_framework-and-folkjs), holographic_rc_implementations)
- Show colour bleeding from walls: Bounce pass re-solves per bounce when the lamp moves; trade-off: one full solve per bounce. ([Examples](../topics/holographic_rc_implementations.md#examples), holographic_rc_implementations)
- Get bounces without temporal feedback: Second cascade set or pass per bounce, or accept one bounce. ([Single-frame assessment](../topics/2d_implementations.md#single-frame-assessment), 2d_implementations)
- Add light bounce to a painted 2D canvas: Field-stepped rays with jump flood; trade-off: noise needs temporal accumulation to hide. ([Part 1: naive GI](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_jason_today.md#part-1-naive-gi), 2d_articles_and_demos_jason_today)
- Add multi-bounce light to world-space RC: Feed last frame's secondary hits into a coarser cache; trade-off: converges over frames. ([Multi-bounce, specular and comparisons](../topics/3d_world_space.md#multi-bounce-specular-and-comparisons), 3d_world_space)
- Light a voxel room with a bounce each frame: Hemisphere probes fed by last frame's irradiance; trade-off: only one new bounce per frame. ([M3ycWt (Mathis): voxel cascades with hemisphere probes](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations_grids.md#m3ycwt-mathis-voxel-cascades-with-hemisphere-probes), 3d_world_space_implementations_grids)
- Get off-screen second bounces without a history cache: World cascades under screen ones, or a per-frame world cache; trade-off is rebuild cost. ([Single-frame assessment](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#single-frame-assessment), 3d_screen_space)
- Add multiple light bounces to screen-space probe GI: Feed last frame's reprojected lighting back as emission; trade-off is one frame of lag. ([What a hit returns](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#what-a-hit-returns), 3d_screen_space)
- Extend Reservoir Cascades from direct emission to multi-bounce GI: Proposed emissive surface cache with one-frame lag; adds bias and lag. ([Limits and design axes](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades.md#limits-and-design-axes), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades)
- Shade multi-bounce paths without a second trace: Multi-bounce shading from last frame's cascade 0; trade-off: colour lost and visible block edges. ([Ray casting and gathering](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#ray-casting-and-gathering), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg)
- Add indirect bounces to cascades without waiting many frames: Previous-frame GI at hits adds one bounce per frame; history carries bounces. ([Shading the hit](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#shading-the-hit), scene_representation_and_tracing)
- Get extra light bounces from probes without extra rays: Previous-iteration shading adds one bounce per frame at a cost independent of frame rate. ([DDGI](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#ddgi), related_probe_gi)
- Add cheap diffuse indirect light to a whole scene each frame: Propagated reflective-shadow-map light in SH grids; cheap, but blurs and leaks through thin walls. ([Light propagation volumes](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#light-propagation-volumes), related_probe_gi)
- Approximate multi-bounce GI with a hashed world cache: Cells keyed by position, normal and LOD; one bounce per frame; bad first samples persist. ([Bevy Solari](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#bevy-solari), related_radiance_caches)
- Get multi-bounce GI at a quarter sample per pixel: Screen probes with 64 rays each, reprojected and blended; needs hardware ray tracing. ([GI-1.0](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#gi-10), related_radiance_caches)
- Trade detail for stability on many-bounce scenes: Trace-probe mode; trade-off: lost detail, switchable per area. ([PC and console path](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#pc-and-console-path), related_engine_gi)
- Add near-field bounce to screen-space AO with one buffer: Reprojected last-frame radiance gives near-field bounce; off-screen light and geometry are lost. ([Horizon-based indirect lighting](../topics/related_screen_space_gi.md#horizon-based-indirect-lighting), related_screen_space_gi)
- Get AO and one-bounce indirect light from one per-slice bitmask: Bitmask gives AO and one-bounce GI; constant thickness leaks at depth steps. ([Visibility-bitmask SSGI](../topics/related_screen_space_gi.md#visibility-bitmask-ssgi), related_screen_space_gi)
- Get full 2D GI in O(N³) from boundary points: Partial-transmittance wave: an unverified discussion claim with no measurements. ([Grid visibility](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#grid-visibility), light_transport_alternatives)
- Get several bounces in one frame without temporal lag: Stored hit distances give N bounces in one frame, opaque scenes only. ([Multi-bounce](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#multi-bounce), light_transport_alternatives)
- Add one diffuse bounce from on-screen lit colour: Lit frame samples weighted by visibility; trade-off: one bounce, limited to visible surfaces. ([Screen-space indirect light (SSDO, SSIL, SSGI)](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#screen-space-indirect-light-ssdo-ssil-ssgi), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Add screen-space occlusion and indirect light without extra geometry: SSAO, HBAO, GTAO and SSGI read the depth buffer; trade-off: only on-screen surfaces count. ([Visibility, shadows and screen space](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#visibility-shadows-and-screen-space), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Converge multi-bounce light in fewer iterations: Mixes recent iterates to shrink the residual; trade-off: stores iterate history per update. ([Anderson acceleration](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#anderson-acceleration), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)

## Reach off-screen and hidden light

Start with [Two families](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#two-families), then [Screen probes with world intervals](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#screen-probes-with-world-intervals) and [Hybrids](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#hybrids). World-space designs that see all geometry are in [Why world space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#why-world-space).

- Reach off-screen light from screen-space probes within a few milliseconds: SPWI with hardware ray tracing; trade-off is tracing takes 87 to 96% of time. ([Screen probes with world intervals (SPWI)](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#screen-probes-with-world-intervals-spwi), single_frame_3d_rc)
- Handle contact light apart from the far field: Cascade −1 as a near-field split; trade-off is a separate near-field path to maintain. ([Connections](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#connections), cascade_layout_and_merging)
- Light scenes from sources beyond the screen: World-space tracing of off-screen light; trade-off: cost grows with the distance covered. ([Off-screen light](../topics/holographic_rc.md#off-screen-light), holographic_rc)
- Get light from off-screen and hidden surfaces into GI: World-space probes trace the scene; trade-off is dense-grid memory. ([Why world space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#why-world-space), 3d_world_space)
- Get world-scale GI off screen while keeping detail up close: Screen probes near, world probes far; trade-off is seams at the handover. ([Hybrids](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#hybrids), 3d_screen_space)
- Catch off-screen emitters without a 3D probe grid's memory: Probes stay on the depth buffer; intervals traced in world space; trade-off is slow rays. ([Screen probes with world intervals](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#screen-probes-with-world-intervals), 3d_screen_space)
- Get off-screen second bounces without a history cache: World cascades under screen ones, or a per-frame world cache; trade-off is rebuild cost. ([Single-frame assessment](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#single-frame-assessment), 3d_screen_space)
- Catch off-screen emitters in screen-space GI: Traces world rays for off-screen light; costs a BVH and misses off-screen second bounces. ([Two families](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#two-families), 3d_screen_space)
- Reach off-screen light in a screen-space probe RC: SPWI traces world-space rays; trade-off is tracing cost, very slow next to screen marching. ([Gavras (2025): SPWI on Metal](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#gavras-2025-spwi-on-metal), 3d_screen_space_theses)
- Light off-screen distance-field hits without shading them: Copy the lit screen into voxels so hits read light; adds lag. ([Radiance at the hit from the lit frame](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#radiance-at-the-hit-from-the-lit-frame), scene_representation_and_tracing)
- Light off-screen hits in the same frame: Direct-light evaluation at each hit; trade-off: extra direct-light cost per hit. ([Single-frame assessment](../topics/godot_integration.md#single-frame-assessment), godot_integration)
- Reach far indirect light with nested coarse grids: Three nested 32³ grids; 8 iterations limit reach; cost is linear in iterations and cells. ([Light propagation volumes](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#light-propagation-volumes), related_probe_gi)
- Light rooms lit through small windows: World radiance cache traces many more rays for distant sky light; trade-off: much higher cost. ([Limits and the 2022 overview](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#limits-and-the-2022-overview), related_engine_gi)
- Catch lights hidden from the camera in screen-space GI: Untested suggestions; trade-off: a second depth map, so extra render cost. ([News and discussion](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#news-and-discussion), history_and_landscape)
- Catch off-screen light without a dense 3D cascade grid: Screen probes with world intervals; trade-off: radiance lives only on depth surfaces. ([Positions on 3D RC](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#positions-on-3d-rc), history_and_landscape)

## Get specular, glossy, reflection and caustic light

Start with [Specular](../topics/core_theory.md#specular), then [Multi-bounce, specular and comparisons](../topics/3d_world_space.md#multi-bounce-specular-and-comparisons) and [Caustics and refraction](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#caustics-and-refraction). Shading terms are in the glossary under [GGX](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#ggx) and [Split-sum approximation](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#split-sum-approximation).

- Answer glossy reflections from cascades: Cone query per cascade; sharp reflections need another method. ([Specular](../topics/core_theory.md#specular), core_theory)
- Keep direction lookups faithful for specular and guiding: Direction-fidelity criterion; trade-off: standard RC's 2^n intervals violate it. ([Derived points](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#derived-points), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg)
- Find reflections of nearby objects from one probe's stored distances: Layered distance maps search one cube map, but miss thin objects when steps exceed texels. ([Layered distance maps](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#layered-distance-maps), scene_representation_and_tracing)
- Smooth noisy probe-based reflections: Bilateral reflection filter; trade-off: blurs detail and is skipped at roughness 0.3 and up. ([HDDAGI](../topics/godot_integration.md#hddagi), godot_integration)
- Cache glossy indirect light per record with spherical harmonics: SH per record with gradients; rotation per lookup is the bottleneck; suits low-frequency glossy BRDFs. ([Radiance caching](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#radiance-caching), related_radiance_caches)
- Keep glossy light from leaking into other directions in a cache: Directional mode: L1 SH luma in YCoCg, keeping glossy light out of other directions. ([SHaRC](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#sharc), related_radiance_caches)
- Render caustics through refraction with cascades; Keep 3D caustic memory bounded: Refractive cascades: residual artifacts, results sensitive to settings. ([Caustics and refraction](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#caustics-and-refraction), light_transport_alternatives)
- Keep single-frame cost constant under caustic splits: Ray doubling at each caustic split breaks the constant-cost property. ([Single-frame assessment](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#single-frame-assessment), light_transport_alternatives)
- Keep glossy interreflection in precomputed GI: Adaptive microbuffer: about twice the memory of plain PBGI, hours offline. ([Wavelet point-based GI](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#wavelet-point-based-gi), light_transport_alternatives)
- Show reflections of visible scene content in real time: Depth-buffer ray tracing reuses on-screen colour; trade-off: only on-screen content reflects. ([Screen-space reflections](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#screen-space-reflections), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Model rough and shiny surfaces with a physical highlight: Long-tail microfacet distribution; trade-off: needs energy compensation at high roughness. ([GGX](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#ggx), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Stop rough microfacet surfaces losing energy: Adds a compensation lobe or scale; trade-off: extra table lookups and shading cost. ([Multiple-scattering energy compensation](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#multiple-scattering-energy-compensation), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Shade glossy reflections from prefiltered environment data: Prefiltered map times BRDF table; trade-off: an approximation rather than an exact integral. ([Split-sum approximation](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#split-sum-approximation), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)

## Light fog, volumes and participating media

Start with [RC as a radiative transfer solver](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#rc-as-a-radiative-transfer-solver), [Integration and merging](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#integration-and-merging) and [Volumetrics in 3D](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#volumetrics-in-3d). Godot's own fog is in [Volumetric fog](../topics/godot_integration.md#volumetric-fog).

- Light a bounded fog volume with single-frame RC: Volumetric RC with far cascades on mips; trade-off is cost at dense 192³ grids. ([Holographic RC and volumetric grids](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#holographic-rc-and-volumetric-grids), single_frame_3d_rc)
- Shorten medical volume RC recalculation at 192³: Cube mapper directions; trade-off is 1.38 to 1.47 times the tetrahedron cost. ([Volumetric and HRC](../topics/performance.md#volumetric-and-hrc), performance)
- Render volumetric light in real time: Extensions from cascade 0; trade-off: README timings do not scale consistently. ([Volumetric-HRC](../topics/holographic_rc_implementations.md#volumetric-hrc), holographic_rc_implementations)
- Map one merge rule onto every volumetric RC variant: One composition rule across codes; trade-off: each code stores different variables. ([Connections](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#connections), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Speed up non-LTE radiative transfer iterations: Sparse mip tracing with per-cascade mip caps; trade-off: peak error near 12% in few cells. ([DexRT](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#dexrt), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Integrate emission and absorption correctly along each interval; Merge media intervals without repeated exponentials; Make glowing gas and opaque emitters both render correctly: Per-step rule with (1−T) factor; needs true step length, not plain step. ([Integration and merging](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#integration-and-merging), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Keep marching steps accurate in inhomogeneous media: Mean-free-path steps or delta tracking; trade-off: large steps skip small lights. ([Marching on mips](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#marching-on-mips), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Make sparse RC work for volumetric fog: Activate sparse probes along rays through fog, or set grid density by fog density; spawning all trilinear neighbours cost about 30% more probes in one volumetric scene. ([Volumetrics in 3D](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#volumetrics-in-3d), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Avoid smeared transmittance when interpolating volumetric probes: Log-space extrapolation; trade-off: no visibility gradient, so tolerance must be lowered. ([Radiance caching in media (Jarosz et al. 2008)](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#radiance-caching-in-media-jarosz-et-al-2008), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Interpolate ray extensions in media without leaking light: Lerp of near and merged far; trade-off: interpolating a discrete field leaks behind crossing rays. ([Ray extensions with media](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#ray-extensions-with-media), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Solve non-LTE radiative transfer for atmospheres with cascades: DexRT's RC formal solver inside an iteration; cost far above real-time. ([RC as a radiative transfer solver](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#rc-as-a-radiative-transfer-solver), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Light CT scans in real time with single-bounce GI: Dense 3D RC field recomputed on change; trade-off: single bounce and bleeding from coarse probes. ([RC for medical volumes (Rulkens)](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#rc-for-medical-volumes-rulkens), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Keep volumetric lighting inside a single-frame budget; Keep fog scattering single-frame except its in-scatter term: Bounded fog volume or screen-plane layout with mips; trade-off: full-scene grids do not fit. ([Single-frame assessment](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#single-frame-assessment), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Turn RGB colours into spectra inside a renderer; Keep spectral throughput from underflowing in single precision; Keep spectral colour noise low with few wavelengths: Sigmoid polynomial uplift; trade-off: 32-bit coefficient table that does not interpolate linearly. ([Spectral transport](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#spectral-transport), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Remove exp bias from noisy optical-depth estimates; Combine distance-sampling strategies in media without bias: Jackknife transmittance from two estimates; trade-off: unbiased only for normally distributed estimates. ([Transmittance estimation (Peters 2025)](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#transmittance-estimation-peters-2025), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Light a volume with radiance cascades in 3D: Full 3D RC; trade-off: one volumetric RC took about half of a 60 fps frame. ([Volumetrics in 3D](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#volumetrics-in-3d), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Model absorbing and emitting media along 2D rays: Per-length absorption ignores step size; tiny sigma falls back to linear emission. ([CPU references and research harnesses](../topics/2d_implementations.md#cpu-references-and-research-harnesses), 2d_implementations)
- Render volumetric light through a cascade stack: Per-sample opacity casts; trade-off: optical depth shifts about 24× across levels (derived). ([Media](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#media), 2d_shadertoys)
- Light fog and participating media with GI: World-space cascades hold radiance off surfaces; trade-off is bounded dense storage. ([Why world space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#why-world-space), 3d_world_space)
- Upscale fog or volume buffers without bleeding over foreground edges: Rotated disc with a depth-spread gate; the disc's TAA purpose is gone in single-frame RC. ([pixelmager's depth-aware upscaling gist](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#pixelmagers-depth-aware-upscaling-gist), 3d_screen_space_implementations)
- Light volumetric fog with custom GI: FogVolume emission route; trade-off: the fog's temporal blend still applies. ([Volumetric fog](../topics/godot_integration.md#volumetric-fog), godot_integration)
- Light fog from GI cheaply: Froxel irradiance with SH1 and temporal blend; trade-off: far slices only sample the grid. ([Volumetric GI](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#volumetric-gi), related_engine_gi)
- Render sky scattering with four wavelengths per pixel: Four-wavelength sampling; fit holds for one atmosphere, aerosols omitted. ([Other uses](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#other-uses), light_transport_alternatives)
- Solve radiative transfer by sweeping with exact sources: Linear-source update is exact per cell; radiation-dependent sources need iteration. ([Sweep solvers for radiative transfer](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#sweep-solvers-for-radiative-transfer), light_transport_alternatives)
- Light participating media in a view-aligned grid: Froxels inject, light and integrate fog front to back; trade-off: coarse cells far away. ([Froxels](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#froxels), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Attenuate light correctly through an absorbing medium: Exponential transmittance over optical depth; trade-off: density must be integrated along each segment. ([Beer-Lambert law](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#beer-lambert-law), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Sample collisions in heterogeneous media without a density bound: Majorant-based flights; trade-off: noise grows when the majorant is loose. ([Delta tracking](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#delta-tracking), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Keep transmittance precise over very thin segments: expm1 avoids cancellation near zero; trade-off: helps only when the argument is small. ([expm1](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#expm1), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Solve transfer where level populations leave equilibrium: Iterates radiation field and statistical equilibrium together; trade-off: iterative cost per step. ([Non-LTE radiative transfer](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#non-lte-radiative-transfer), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Keep absorption unchanged when march step length changes: Rescales opacity by step ratio; trade-off: assumes one medium along the step. ([Opacity correction](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#opacity-correction), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Model light scattering in fog and clouds: Henyey-Greenstein lobe set by g; trade-off: one parameter cannot match every medium. ([Phase functions](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#phase-functions), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Solve transfer through media with fixed directions: Discrete ordinates sum over fixed directions; trade-off: accuracy depends on the direction set. ([Radiative transfer equation and discrete ordinates](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#radiative-transfer-equation-and-discrete-ordinates), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Solve transport on a grid with upwind interpolation: Upstream intensity interpolated along short segments; trade-off: needs interpolation at every grid point. ([Short characteristics](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#short-characteristics), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)

## Light from the sky and the sun

Start with [Sky and environment](../topics/core_theory.md#sky-and-environment) and [Sky aliasing](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#sky-aliasing).

- Keep a small sun from aliasing in the top cascade: Analytic sky integral per bin; no closed form in 3D. ([Sky and environment](../topics/core_theory.md#sky-and-environment), core_theory)
- Keep sun shadows sharp in an RC scene: Shadow direct light separately; RC alone blurs sun penumbras at 4 to 5 cascades. ([Which distance: occluder and light separated](../topics/core_theory.md#which-distance-occluder-and-light-separated), core_theory)
- Stop a small sun from aliasing in the top cascade: Integrate the sky per bin; more shader work than point sampling. ([Sky aliasing](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#sky-aliasing), artifacts_and_fixes)
- Add sky and sun to a 2D RC without aliasing: Sky-bin integral: a small sun does not alias; only top-level misses use it. ([Unity and other ports](../topics/2d_implementations.md#unity-and-other-ports), 2d_implementations)
- Shade a heightmap with sun visibility and occlusion: Horizon map from row scans; trade-off: no horizon along y and O(W) per pixel. ([Shaders that are not RC](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#shaders-that-are-not-rc), 2d_shadertoys)
- Light rooms lit through small windows: World radiance cache traces many more rays for distant sky light; trade-off: much higher cost. ([Limits and the 2022 overview](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#limits-and-the-2022-overview), related_engine_gi)
- Light terrain from a moving sun without tracing rays: One-axis horizon map gives sun-disc visibility; only +/-x slopes are stored. ([Heightmap horizons](../topics/related_screen_space_gi.md#heightmap-horizons), related_screen_space_gi)
- Render sky scattering with four wavelengths per pixel: Four-wavelength sampling; fit holds for one atmosphere, aerosols omitted. ([Other uses](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#other-uses), light_transport_alternatives)
- Store environment directions in a rectangular map: Latitude-longitude map; trade-off: rows near poles need sin θ weighting. ([Latitude-longitude map](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#latitude-longitude-map), supporting_techniques)
