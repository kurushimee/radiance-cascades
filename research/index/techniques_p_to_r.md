# Techniques: P to R

Named techniques, algorithms, data structures, encodings, artifacts and concepts, in alphabetical order. Each entry gives a one-line description, the sections that explain it, and the other topics that use it. ## P

- **Parabolic maps**: Front and back hemispheres via x/(1±z), y/(1±z); view-independent, no singularity. [supporting_techniques: Parabolic maps](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#parabolic-maps). Also in [related_radiance_caches](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#radiance-caching).
- **Parallax-based interval bound**: Bound θ_min = 2·arcsin(l/(2·t_max)) from the cell's inscribed circle. [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg: Parallax-based interval bounds](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#parallax-based-interval-bounds).
- **Parallax fix**: Keeps the vanilla ray, reprojects each upper direction, then blends bilinearly. [artifacts_and_fixes: Straight-ray fixes](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#straight-ray-fixes), [2d_shadertoys: Diagrams and helpers](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#diagrams-and-helpers). Also in [2d_articles_and_demos](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#max-coppen-fundamentals-of-radiance-cascades), [2d_implementations](../topics/2d_implementations.md#godot), [light_transport_alternatives](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#line-sweeping), [cascade_layout_and_merging](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#overlap), [performance](../topics/performance.md#what-the-numbers-show), [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#community-site-wiki-and-tools), [holographic_rc](../topics/holographic_rc.md#what-it-fixes).
- **Parallax in bin units (derived)**: Child-to-parent misalignment in bins; constant only on equal-error rows. [core_theory: Parallax in bin units (derived)](../topics/core_theory.md#parallax-in-bin-units-derived). Also in [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding.md#connections).
- **Path guiding**: Learns incident radiance during rendering; samples directions from it. [supporting_techniques: Path guiding](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#path-guiding). Also in [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding.md#vlam), [related_probe_gi](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#ddgi-resampling).
- **Path space filtering**: Averages path vertices that share a quantized hash key, with no neighbour search. [related_radiance_caches: Path space filtering](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#path-space-filtering). Also in [3d_world_space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#split-rc-against-ddgi-and-a-reference).
- **PCG and GPU hashes**: 64-bit LCG with an output permutation that hides its weak low bits. [supporting_techniques: PCG and GPU hashes](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#pcg-and-gpu-hashes).
- **Pentalinear interpolation**: 32-tap trilinear over probes times bilinear over directions. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Multilinear interpolation](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#multilinear-interpolation).
- **Penumbra condition**: Spatial step grows with distance while angular step shrinks as 1/D. [core_theory: The penumbra condition](../topics/core_theory.md#the-penumbra-condition), [3d_world_space_implementations: anoptic-engine](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#anoptic-engine), [3d_screen_space: Interval units and view depth](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#interval-units-and-view-depth), [3d_screen_space_theses: Gavras (2025): SPWI on Metal](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#gavras-2025-spwi-on-metal). Also in [related_screen_space_gi](../topics/related_screen_space_gi.md#connections), [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding.md#cascades-and-light-sampling), [3d_world_space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#split-rc-against-ddgi-and-a-reference), [related_engine_gi](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#connections), [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#the-paper), [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#parallax-based-interval-bounds), [holographic_rc](../topics/holographic_rc.md#geometry), [related_radiance_caches](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#connections).
- **Penumbra hypothesis**: A discretization meeting the penumbra condition represents any radiance field well. [core_theory: The penumbra hypothesis](../topics/core_theory.md#the-penumbra-hypothesis), [2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders: GM Shaders (Yaazarai and Xor)](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders.md#gm-shaders-yaazarai-and-xor).
- **Per-cascade mip marching**: Cascade N marches mip N (or N−1, N−2) with step 2^mip; steps per cascade constant. [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer: Marching on mips](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#marching-on-mips). Also in [core_theory](../topics/core_theory.md#rays-traced-and-rays-built).
- **Per-interval transmittance**: RC stores visibility per interval, so far light merges only through what nearer intervals pass. [related_probe_gi: Connections](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#connections). Also in [related_radiance_caches](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#connections).
- **Permutation sampling**: Reads temporal history at a permuted pixel offset to decorrelate neighbours. [supporting_techniques: Permutation sampling](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#permutation-sampling), [related_radiance_caches: kajiya](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#kajiya).
- **Persistent threads**: Threads fill the GPU once and pull rays from an atomic-counter pool. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Aila and Laine traversal](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#aila-and-laine-traversal).
- **Peter-panning**: Failure mode where too much bias detaches shadows from their casters. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Shadow maps, acne and bias](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#shadow-maps-acne-and-bias).
- **Phantom walls**: Failure mode where thick slabs make rays hit surfaces behind silhouettes. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Depth thickness](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#depth-thickness).
- **Phase functions**: Angular distribution of light scattering in a medium. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Phase functions](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#phase-functions). Also in [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#volumetrics-in-3d).
- **Ping-pong passes**: Any pass reading what another dispatch of the same pass writes needs swapped buffers. [godot_projects: Checklist from these projects](../topics/godot_projects.md#checklist-from-these-projects), [related_probe_gi: Light propagation volumes](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#light-propagation-volumes).
- **Plane cascades**: Sannikov's 2D variant with probes on vertical planes 8 px apart and doubling directions. [holographic_rc: Plane cascades](../topics/holographic_rc.md#plane-cascades).
- **PLOC**: Bottom-up BVH build merging mutually nearest clusters in a Morton-sorted window. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: BVH](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#bvh).
- **Point-based GI**: Stores outgoing radiance on a hierarchy of surface points. [light_transport_alternatives: Wavelet point-based GI](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#wavelet-point-based-gi).
- **Polygonal cascades**: Tiles space with non-overlapping polygons; near-exact falloff with fewer directions. [holographic_rc: Polygonal cascades](../topics/holographic_rc.md#polygonal-cascades).
- **Position-first layout**: One probe's directions form a tile; merge reads 16 texels per lower ray. [cascade_layout_and_merging: Position-first and direction-first](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#position-first-and-direction-first), [2d_articles_and_demos: Otavio Peixoto, OPEngine](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#otavio-peixoto-opengine). Also in [2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders.md#gm-shaders-yaazarai-and-xor).
- **Pre-averaging**: Each stored texel holds the mean of the rays it will merge into below. [cascade_layout_and_merging: Pre-averaging](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#pre-averaging), [core_theory: Rays cast against directions stored](../topics/core_theory.md#rays-cast-against-directions-stored), [2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders: GM Shaders (Yaazarai and Xor)](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders.md#gm-shaders-yaazarai-and-xor), [3d_world_space_implementations: Merge and temporal reuse](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#merge-and-temporal-reuse). Also in [2d_implementations](../topics/2d_implementations.md#bevy), [light_transport_alternatives](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#connections), [3d_screen_space](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#parameters-across-projects), [3d_screen_space_theses](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#gavras-2025-spwi-on-metal), [3d_surface_probes](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#cascades-and-probes), [related_screen_space_gi](../topics/related_screen_space_gi.md#open-questions), [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding.md#connections), [artifacts_and_fixes](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#the-bilinear-fix).
- **Prefiltered depth moments**: Only single-frame fix found for SSRC stability. [3d_screen_space: Single-frame assessment](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#single-frame-assessment). Also in [single_frame_3d_rc](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#screen-space-rc).
- **Prefix sums and decoupled look-back**: Tiles publish aggregates and look back over predecessors' status, about 2n traffic. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Prefix sums and decoupled look-back](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#prefix-sums-and-decoupled-look-back), [3d_world_space: GPUSorting](../topics/3d_world_space.md#gpusorting), [3d_surface_probes: Hash grid (`shared/hash.slang`)](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#hash-grid-sharedhashslang). Also in [performance](../topics/performance.md#gpu-building-blocks).
- **Premultiplied alpha and "over"**: Premultiplied colour keeps over compositing and filtering linear and associative. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Premultiplied alpha and "over"](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#premultiplied-alpha-and-over).
- **Probe flicker under motion**: Small movements flicker every artifact; worst at low settings. [3d_screen_space_theses: Abou Dan and Jensen (2025): visibility-bitmask screen probes](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#abou-dan-and-jensen-2025-visibility-bitmask-screen-probes), [3d_screen_space_theses: Kung (2025): RC against voxel cone tracing](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#kung-2025-rc-against-voxel-cone-tracing). Also in [single_frame_3d_rc](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#screen-probes-with-world-intervals-spwi).
- **Probe relocation**: Moves probes out of geometry, as DDGI does; erhe leaves probes where they sit. [3d_world_space_implementations: Measurements against DDGI](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#measurements-against-ddgi), [related_probe_gi: DDGI](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#ddgi).
- **Probe visibility test**: Chebyshev test on stored mean and squared distance, keeping probes from leaking through walls. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Variance shadow maps and the Chebyshev bound](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#variance-shadow-maps-and-the-chebyshev-bound).
- **Pull-back on far reads**: Cheapest engine-GI leak defence, applied at each interval end. [single_frame_3d_rc: Leaks without history](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#leaks-without-history). Also in [related_engine_gi](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#single-frame-assessment).
- **Pull-push**: Pull averages known texels into coarser levels; push fills gaps from above. [supporting_techniques: Pull-push](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#pull-push).

## Q

- **Quad shuffles**: quadSwapX and quadSwapY average four ray threads; about 10% gain on the ray pass. [3d_screen_space: World rays](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#world-rays). Also in [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#subgroup-operations).
- **Quadrant sweeps**: Applies the same 1D sweep to four rotated grids, then one integrate pass. [holographic_rc_implementations: Quadrant sweeps](../topics/holographic_rc_implementations.md#quadrant-sweeps).
- **Quadrilinear interpolation**: Trilinear in space plus linear between two mip levels, as cone tracing samples. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Multilinear interpolation](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#multilinear-interpolation). Also in [related_probe_gi](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#voxel-cone-tracing).
- **Quarter-strength Lambert term**: Divides by ray count with no 1/π, a quarter of Lambertian shading. [3d_screen_space_implementations: ClaikoCode/DX12RadianceCascades](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#claikocodedx12radiancecascades). Also in [3d_screen_space](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#final-gather).

## R

- **R11G11B10F**: Three unsigned floats in 32 bits, 5-bit exponents, with no sign or alpha. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: R11G11B10F](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#r11g11b10f), [cascade_layout_and_merging: Formats](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#formats), [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer: Storage and colour](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#storage-and-colour). Also in [related_probe_gi](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#ddgi).
- **R2**: Additive recurrence with plastic-number constant; every prefix well spread. [supporting_techniques: R2](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#r2).
- **Radial HRC**: Earlier community test of small perpendicular spacing in HRC. [history_and_landscape: Community site, wiki and tools](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#community-site-wiki-and-tools). Also in [holographic_rc](../topics/holographic_rc.md#equal-angle-ring-radial-and-axis-aligned-hrc).
- **Radiance caching**: Caches incoming radiance per record as spherical harmonics, for glossy surfaces. [related_radiance_caches: Radiance caching](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#radiance-caching).
- **Radiance caching in media (Jarosz et al. 2008)**: Records store radiance and gradient; reused within a gradient-set radius. [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer: Radiance caching in media (Jarosz et al. 2008)](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#radiance-caching-in-media-jarosz-et-al-2008).
- **Radiance cascades**: Probe hierarchy whose coarse and fine levels are merged to give GI. [history_and_landscape: The paper](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#the-paper). Also in [related_radiance_caches](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#bevy-solari).
- **Radiance field stored on a plane**: Path-traced volume's light stored as one cross-section plane; renders in under 0.5 ms. [3d_surface_probes: A radiance field stored on a plane](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#a-radiance-field-stored-on-a-plane).
- **Radiance grid**: Up to four clips of 32×32×d probes, each with 8×8 octahedral radiance texels. [related_engine_gi: Radiance grid](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#radiance-grid).
- **Radiance interval**: Radiance from distances [a, b] along a ray; zero behind an opaque hit. [core_theory: Radiance intervals](../topics/core_theory.md#radiance-intervals), [history_and_landscape: The paper](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#the-paper).
- **Radiative transfer equation and discrete ordinates**: Replaces the angular integral with a weighted sum over fixed directions. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Radiative transfer equation and discrete ordinates](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#radiative-transfer-equation-and-discrete-ordinates). Also in [core_theory](../topics/core_theory.md#connections).
- **Radiometric quantities**: Radiance, irradiance and fluence, the last integrated over all directions without a cosine. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Radiometric quantities](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#radiometric-quantities).
- **Radix sort and Onesweep**: Radix sort variant folding each digit's scan into one decoupled look-back pass. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Radix sort and Onesweep](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#radix-sort-and-onesweep), [3d_world_space: GPUSorting](../topics/3d_world_space.md#gpusorting). Also in [performance](../topics/performance.md#gpu-building-blocks).
- **Radon and Hough transforms, projection-slice theorem**: The 1D Fourier transform of a projection is a central slice of the 2D transform. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Radon and Hough transforms, projection-slice theorem](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#radon-and-hough-transforms-projection-slice-theorem). Also in [light_transport_alternatives](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#solutions-without-occlusion).
- **Ray queries**: Lets any shader stage, compute included, traverse an acceleration structure and read hits. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Ray queries](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#ray-queries). Also in [single_frame_3d_rc](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#tracing-and-scene-representation-under-renderingdevice), [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding.md#single-frame-assessment).
- **Ray sorting**: Rays binned by position and octahedral direction, with hits shaded by separate shaders. [related_probe_gi: Enshrouded](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#enshrouded). Also in [cascade_layout_and_merging](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#connections), [related_radiance_caches](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#connections).
- **Ray splitting**: One ray per pixel tells each cascade whether its interval is open or hit. [3d_world_space: Ray splitting](../topics/3d_world_space.md#ray-splitting), [single_frame_3d_rc: Sparse world probes near surfaces (Split RC)](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#sparse-world-probes-near-surfaces-split-rc), [performance: Budgets for single-frame 3D RC (derived)](../topics/performance.md#budgets-for-single-frame-3d-rc-derived). Also in [3d_surface_probes](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#connections), [godot_integration](../topics/godot_integration.md#connections).
- **Rays traced and rays built**: Stored intervals are traced; merges build K^N full-length rays. [core_theory: Rays traced and rays built](../topics/core_theory.md#rays-traced-and-rays-built).
- **RC-guided NEE**: Wiki category for pathtracers where RC augments ray tracing; vlam is filed under it. [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding: vlam](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding.md#vlam). Also in [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#community-site-wiki-and-tools).
- **RCPG**: Path guiding that prefers the octahedral map because equal-area maps need transcendental maths. [cascade_layout_and_merging: Direction maps in 3D](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#direction-maps-in-3d). Also in [performance](../topics/performance.md#related-techniques).
- **Reduce Demand**: jason.today's two-frame mode; its first frame is never read. [2d_articles_and_demos: Single-frame assessment](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#single-frame-assessment). Also in [2d_articles_and_demos_jason_today](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_jason_today.md#part-2-building-rc).
- **Reduce into the probe field**: Cosine-convolves merged radiance into 6×6 irradiance and 14×14 distance maps, DDGI format. [3d_world_space_implementations: Reduce into the probe field](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#reduce-into-the-probe-field).
- **Reflective shadow map**: Per-light depth, normal and flux map; each texel becomes a virtual point light. [related_probe_gi: Light propagation volumes](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#light-propagation-volumes). Also in [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#other-work-on-sannikovs-channel).
- **Reinhard global operator**: L/(1 + L) after scaling by the key over the log-average luminance. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Tone mapping](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#tone-mapping).
- **Resampled importance sampling (RIS)**: Draw M candidates, weight by target over source, keep one in proportion. [supporting_techniques: Resampled importance sampling (RIS)](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#resampled-importance-sampling-ris). Also in [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding.md#methods-compared-and-cited), [related_radiance_caches](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#bevy-solari).
- **ReSTIR**: Streams candidates into reservoirs, merges neighbours spatially and over time. [supporting_techniques: ReSTIR](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#restir). Also in [3d_surface_probes](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#on-surface-radiance-caches-tatzgern-et-al-2024), [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#adoption-shipped-tried-and-turned-down), [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#rcpg-product-path-guiding-with-radiance-cascades), [related_probe_gi](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#ddgi-resampling), [related_radiance_caches](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#bevy-solari).
- **ReSTIR GI**: Half-resolution ReSTIR for the first bounce, with reservoirs reused in time and space. [related_radiance_caches: kajiya](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#kajiya). Also in [related_engine_gi](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#pc-and-console-path), [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#evaluation), [supporting_techniques](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#restir).
- **Reversed-smoothstep old sun**: Old sun: smoothstep(1, 0, φ) with reversed edges, used when Naive GI Noise is on. [2d_articles_and_demos: radiance-cascades.com demo and Osborne's slide demo](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#radiance-cascadescom-demo-and-osbornes-slide-demo). Also in [2d_articles_and_demos_jason_today](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_jason_today.md#part-2-building-rc).
- **Reversed Z**: Near plane maps to 1, far to 0 in float depth, balancing precision against 1/z. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Reversed Z](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#reversed-z), [3d_screen_space_implementations: three-rc](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#three-rc), [godot_integration: Buffers in a callback](../topics/godot_integration.md#buffers-in-a-callback).
- **RGB10A2**: Three 10-bit unorm channels and a 2-bit alpha, DXGI format 24. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: RGB10A2](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#rgb10a2). Also in [cascade_layout_and_merging](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#formats), [related_probe_gi](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#ddgi).
- **RGB9E5**: Three 9-bit mantissas sharing one 5-bit exponent, non-negative, with no alpha. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: RGB9E5](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#rgb9e5). Also in [cascade_layout_and_merging](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#formats), [holographic_rc](../topics/holographic_rc.md#artifacts-and-fixes).
- **RGBA16F**: Radiance with continuous transmittance; the only portable filterable float target on the web. [cascade_layout_and_merging: Formats](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#formats). Also in [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#storage-and-colour).
