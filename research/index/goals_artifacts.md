## Remove ringing and merge artifacts

Start with [Ringing](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#ringing) for the mechanism, then [the bilinear fix](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#the-bilinear-fix), the [straight-ray fixes](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#straight-ray-fixes) and [cheaper fixes](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#cheaper-fixes), compared in [Fixes compared](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#fixes-compared). The kernels themselves are in [Merge kernels](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#merge-kernels), and the theory of where merging is exact is in [Shift and extend](../topics/core_theory.md#shift-and-extend).

- Remove ringing and leaks in merging with a bilinear fix: Blends four upper probes before weighting; trade-off is four times the rays. ([Merge kernels](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#merge-kernels), cascade_layout_and_merging)
- Close light leaks at cascade boundaries without causing ringing: Overlap of about √2 upper spacings; trade-off is halos and double-counted partial absorbers. ([Overlap](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#overlap), cascade_layout_and_merging)
- Keep bilinear upper-probe weights consistent across cascades: Cell-centred probes with the bilinear fix; trade-off is one shared ray-offset convention. ([Probe placement](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#probe-placement), cascade_layout_and_merging)
- Run base-16 ray counts without artifacts: base-B scaling cuts the spatial ratio ×0.25 per cascade at base 16; `modifierHack` (√B) retunes interval length to base 4's cascade 0 balance. ([Base ray counts other than 4](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#base-ray-counts-other-than-4), artifacts_and_fixes)
- Remove ringing and leaks at lower cost than bilinear: Nearest fix with forking adds no rays; penumbras turn blocky. ([Cheaper fixes](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#cheaper-fixes), artifacts_and_fixes)
- Stop solids from ringing or darkening at cascade starts: Interior mask returns black above cascade 0; darkens at 512 squared. ([Rays starting inside geometry](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#rays-starting-inside-geometry), artifacts_and_fixes)
- Fix ringing without tracing four rays per probe: Circle-intersection fix is about 3x faster; undefined on cascades 0 and 1. ([Straight-ray fixes](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#straight-ray-fixes), artifacts_and_fixes)
- Remove ringing and leaks at cascade boundaries: Bilinear fix traces four rays per probe; costs about 4x the rays. ([The bilinear fix](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#the-bilinear-fix), artifacts_and_fixes)
- Fix crosses around small or enclosed emitters: Trace the first 3 or 4 cascades; trade-off: more tracing cost than extensions. ([Artifacts and fixes](../topics/holographic_rc.md#artifacts-and-fixes), holographic_rc)
- Remove crosses by giving frusta equal angular width: Equal-angle frusta; trade-off: refraction across quadrant boundaries still errs. ([Equal-angle, ring, radial and axis-aligned HRC](../topics/holographic_rc.md#equal-angle-ring-radial-and-axis-aligned-hrc), holographic_rc)
- Remove checkerboard from probe rows: Blur or drop rows so odd and even lattices merge; trade-off: blur or lost resolution. ([Final gather and cleanup](../topics/holographic_rc.md#final-gather-and-cleanup), holographic_rc)
- Remove crosses and X patterns from diagonal light: Atan cone weights and half-integer tangents; weights must be applied per cone, not after merging. ([Merging down](../topics/holographic_rc.md#merging-down), holographic_rc)
- Avoid child-index mismatch between direction cascades: Quadtree (Morton) layout; trade-off: row-major cube faces break the 4a+l child rule. ([RC for CT volumes (repository)](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#rc-for-ct-volumes-repository), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Reduce light leaking in the merge step: Bilinear-fix merge adds visibility checks, at higher ray cost. ([TypeGPU (Software Mansion)](../topics/2d_implementations.md#typegpu-software-mansion), 2d_implementations)
- Remove occlusion errors from interpolated cascades: Bilinear fix blends four upper fetches; trade-off: four times more raymarches per texel. ([GM Shaders (Yaazarai and Xor)](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders.md#gm-shaders-yaazarai-and-xor), 2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders)
- Understand the parallax fix's reprojected lookup: Reprojected per-probe lookup; trade-off: two of four circle roots go negative, derived. ([Diagrams and helpers](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#diagrams-and-helpers), 2d_shadertoys)
- Hide ringing around small lights in a cascade merge: Four-probe bilinear fix hides ringing; trade-off: the author calls it nonphysical. ([Media](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#media), 2d_shadertoys)
- Compare ringing fixes inside one testbed framework: MERGE_FIX modes selecting bilinear, midpoint, Dolkar or parallax merges; bilinear costs 16 traces per texel. ([The testbed line](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#the-testbed-line), 2d_shadertoys)
- Remove merge artifacts from plain bilinear probe interpolation: Bilateral or bilinear 3D merge; bilateral shows striations at steep angles. ([Gavras (2025): SPWI on Metal](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#gavras-2025-spwi-on-metal), 3d_screen_space_theses)
- Keep merges correct at depth edges and under parent-child parallax: Bilinear fix with bilinear 3D weights; trade-off is extra traces and a two-frame refresh. ([scy3Dd: SPWI in one shader](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#scy3dd-spwi-in-one-shader), 3d_screen_space_implementations)
- Remove ringing from deterministic ray placement across frames: Block-consistent jitter turns boundary bands into variance; frame averaging removes them. ([Method](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades.md#method), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades)
- Stop ringing from deterministic ray placement in RC: Turning the merge into resampling makes ringing variance that frame averaging can remove. ([The argument](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades.md#the-argument), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades)
- Bound reuse bias across all cascade levels: Sum of ε_n stays under 3·ε0 at any depth, given bounded radiance. ([Theory](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades.md#theory), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades)
- Resample cascades without stripe artifacts: Footprint integration beats point sampling; stripes need cascade oversampling. ([Resampling view of RC](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#resampling-view-of-rc), light_transport_alternatives)
- Remove bilinear artifacts from cascade merging: Trace each lower probe to its four upper probes; trade-off: more merge work per probe. ([First public implementations and the Shadertoy lineage](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#first-public-implementations-and-the-shadertoy-lineage), history_and_landscape)
- Weight intervals with band-limited kernels that bound error: Majorant interval weights with minimal integral error; trade-off: overshoot near interval edges. ([Beurling-Selberg functions](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#beurling-selberg-functions), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Know where the circle fix is undefined: A ray that starts outside the circle and points away from it has no forward exit, so the interval must be at least `2.12·Δs_c` long in 2D and `2.60·Δs_c` in 3D; every code that uses the fix fails this on its lowest cascades. ([Straight-ray fixes](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#straight-ray-fixes), artifacts_and_fixes)

## Stop light leaks

Start with [Light leaks](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#light-leaks), then [Leaks without history](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#leaks-without-history) for the single-frame view and [Leak defences in world space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#leak-defences-in-world-space) for 3D grids. Visibility tests used against leaks are [variance shadow maps and the Chebyshev bound](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#variance-shadow-maps-and-the-chebyshev-bound) and [moment shadow maps](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#moment-shadow-maps-and-power-moments).

- Stop light leaking through walls in a single-frame merge: Parent hit-distance test with hit mask; trade-off is about 50% more merge time. ([Leaks without history](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#leaks-without-history), single_frame_3d_rc)
- Stop world-space leaks without the 4.4× trilinear fix: A cubic grid split into tetrahedra, 2 or 3 rays per lower ray; trade-off: about 1.85× in time, and in 3D the free parent hit-distance test needs child-to-parent visibility. ([Where the evidence stops](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#where-the-evidence-stops), single_frame_3d_rc)
- Keep cascade 0 contact shadows and leaks exact: Exact near structure for cascade 0; trade-off is the cost of that exact structure. ([Tracing and scene representation under `RenderingDevice`](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#tracing-and-scene-representation-under-renderingdevice), single_frame_3d_rc)
- Remove ringing and leaks in merging with a bilinear fix: Blends four upper probes before weighting; trade-off is four times the rays. ([Merge kernels](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#merge-kernels), cascade_layout_and_merging)
- Close light leaks at cascade boundaries without causing ringing: Overlap of about √2 upper spacings; trade-off is halos and double-counted partial absorbers. ([Overlap](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#overlap), cascade_layout_and_merging)
- Remove ringing and leaks at lower cost than bilinear: Nearest fix with forking adds no rays; penumbras turn blocky. ([Cheaper fixes](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#cheaper-fixes), artifacts_and_fixes)
- Stop light leaking through walls thinner than probe spacing; Stop leaks along direction-tile edges: Bilinear fix helps, otherwise a finer base grid; the fix costs about 4x rays. ([Light leaks](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#light-leaks), artifacts_and_fixes)
- Remove ringing and leaks at cascade boundaries: Bilinear fix traces four rays per probe; costs about 4x the rays. ([The bilinear fix](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#the-bilinear-fix), artifacts_and_fixes)
- Keep the final blur from crossing thin occluders: TypeGPU blurs a pixel only with neighbours whose opacity differs by less than 0.1 in every channel, so it blurs across an occluder whose opacity is that close to its surroundings. ([Integrate and source passes](../topics/holographic_rc_implementations.md#integrate-and-source-passes), holographic_rc_implementations)
- Stop light leaking through thin walls in coarse mips: Directional opacity, six per voxel; trade-off: about 6× opacity storage. ([Marching on mips](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#marching-on-mips), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Keep thin walls closed in coarse 3D mips: Six directional opacities, a variance limit, or max-filtering; trade-off: storage, or lost energy in media. ([Marching on mips](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#marching-on-mips), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Interpolate ray extensions in media without leaking light: Lerp of near and merged far; trade-off: interpolating a discrete field leaks behind crossing rays. ([Ray extensions with media](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#ray-extensions-with-media), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Stop single fetches leaking across cascades: pow(2, i²) per-cascade weights; they track a mip's dilution of thin occluders and fit three cascades, over-weighting a fourth. ([Setups that worked in 2D](../topics/2d_implementations.md#setups-that-worked-in-2d), 2d_implementations)
- Reduce light leaking in the merge step: Bilinear-fix merge adds visibility checks, at higher ray cost. ([TypeGPU (Software Mansion)](../topics/2d_implementations.md#typegpu-software-mansion), 2d_implementations)
- Stop light leaking through walls thinner than probe spacing: Gate parents or start rays from receiving surfaces; trade-off: extra rays or stored bits. ([Leak defences in world space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#leak-defences-in-world-space), 3d_world_space)
- Stop light leaking through walls between cascade probes: per_neighbour_trace drops upper probes behind walls; trade-off: up to 8 extra rays per texel. ([Merge modes](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#merge-modes), 3d_world_space_implementations)
- Catch light leaks through walls before shipping a probe layout: Leak gates on paired rooms and offset sweeps; trade-off: thresholds tied to erhe's own scenes. ([Stations and gates](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#stations-and-gates), 3d_world_space_implementations)
- Trace thin objects in a depth march without leaks: Per-sector masks or moment prefiltering; trade-off is extra storage and merge complexity. ([Depth-buffer marching](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#depth-buffer-marching), 3d_screen_space)
- Stop light and shadow leaking across depth edges in merges: Bilinear 3D weights follow the surface; trade-off is a few iterations and zero-length edge risk. ([Merging across depth edges](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#merging-across-depth-edges), 3d_screen_space)
- Remove splotchy leaks at depth edges in screen-space probe RC: Min+Max probes; trade-off is 2 to 17% more time and twice the memory. ([Kaméus (2025): min/max probe placement](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#kaméus-2025-minmax-probe-placement), 3d_screen_space_theses)
- Merge coarser-cascade light only where its hit point is visible: Hit-point reprojection with a visibility window; trade-off is a 5×5 gather per texel. ([lXByRh: hit-point reprojection](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#lxbyrh-hit-point-reprojection), 3d_screen_space_implementations)
- Keep directional occlusion within a screen-space RC interval: Per-sector masks replace scalar openness; trade-off is 32-bit masks per interval and sector bookkeeping. ([Unreal: screen-space bitmask RC](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#unreal-screen-space-bitmask-rc), 3d_screen_space_implementations)
- Stop light leaking through gaps between surfels; Keep indoor pixels from reading outdoor probes: Transparent outer rings; overlapping hits blend albedo by power moments, approximating overlap. ([Artifacts and fixes](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#artifacts-and-fixes-1), 3d_surface_probes)
- Stop probe leaks without extra visibility rays or bits: Hit-distance parent test reuses stored hit distances; trade-off: in 3D a parent across a wall passes it, so off-surface parents need child-to-parent visibility. ([Connections](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#connections), 3d_surface_probes)
- Stop light leaking through thin walls in real-time GI: Surface hemisphere caches on mesh islands; seams at island borders, 300-700 MB per viewer. ([On-surface radiance caches (Tatzgern et al. 2024)](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#on-surface-radiance-caches-tatzgern-et-al-2024), 3d_surface_probes)
- Cut leaks while keeping small-emitter energy at equal ray budget: At rho 0.375, leak is 15.8% of vanilla for at most 20% extra rays. ([Experiments](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades.md#experiments), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades)
- Pick a validation rate that trades leak against shadow-ray cost: Higher rho removes leak but adds 33 to 53% shadow rays and costs small-emitter recovery. ([Limits and design axes](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades.md#limits-and-design-axes), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades)
- Keep light leaks through thin occluders out of RC merges: Renormalized validation drops blocked parents; each unit of rho adds 32 to 53% extra rays. ([Method](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades.md#method), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades)
- Remove light leaks through thin occluders in RC: Resampling turns interpolation bias into variance that reuse can amortize; validation adds shadow rays. ([The argument](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades.md#the-argument), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades)
- Stop thin walls from leaking in a distance field; Hand rays from screen traces to distance fields without leaks: Surface expansion closes thin walls but over-occludes, so GI rays grow it with distance. ([Lumen: mesh and global distance fields](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#lumen-mesh-and-global-distance-fields), scene_representation_and_tracing)
- Keep thin walls opaque in averaged voxel mips: Six-direction opacity keeps wall opacity; builds measured speed, not leaks. ([Voxel tracing measured for RC](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs.md#voxel-tracing-measured-for-rc), scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs)
- Stop probe interpolation from leaking through walls: Visibility-tested merges in world-space RC; trade-off: a merge cost paid per probe. ([Connections](../topics/godot_integration.md#connections), godot_integration)
- Reduce light leaks at probe corners: Proposed thicker walls, denser probes or depth-aware blur; each costs memory or sharpness. ([HDDAGI](../topics/godot_integration.md#hddagi), godot_integration)
- Stop thin walls and small emitters leaking or vanishing: Visibility merge drops blocked corners; trade-off: opaque-black fallback darkens near thin walls. ([Limitations and discrepancies](../topics/godot_projects.md#limitations-and-discrepancies), godot_projects)
- Stop probes inside walls and ceilings from leaking light: Backface zeroing and a normal-shifted lookup; corners still leak with tangent-plane weights. ([Chen's Monter probes](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#chens-monter-probes), related_probe_gi)
- Keep probe light from leaking through walls: Chebyshev visibility and wrapped backface weights; too much bias leaks through walls. ([DDGI](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#ddgi), related_probe_gi)
- Keep probes that land in walls from lighting the wrong side: Push-out to the nearest SDF surface, then the dead-probe test removes probes under 1 mm. ([Enshrouded](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#enshrouded), related_probe_gi)
- Remove light leaks around occluders from probe lighting: Visibility-aware irradiance: eight texture fetches per point; noise where visibility is complex. ([Light-field probes](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#light-field-probes), related_probe_gi)
- Stop a leaked record's wrong value spreading to neighbours: Neighbour clamping spreads spacing smoothly, but cannot fix a leaked record's own value. ([Fixes from production](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#fixes-from-production), related_radiance_caches)
- Separate dark interiors from bright exteriors in a shared cache: Ray-length key bit splits cells so dark and bright hits do not share one. ([GI-1.0](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#gi-10), related_radiance_caches)
- Keep thin sunlit walls from leaking light into rooms: Random origin per cell and rank-gated votes stop cache-only rays leaking outside. ([kajiya](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#kajiya), related_radiance_caches)
- Keep indirect diffuse cheap across a large scene without leaks: Radiance's adaptive sampling and leak tests; bias from spacing limits and assumed reflectance. ([Radiance's practice](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#radiances-practice), related_radiance_caches)
- Stop light leaks through probe interpolation: Pull-back and plane tests; trade-off: interpolation only from probes on the pixel's plane. ([Connections](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#connections), related_engine_gi)
- Stop light bleeding at voxel volume borders: Convex offset filtering; trade-off: leaks remain where fill data or opacity is missing. ([daGI v1 in Enlisted (2019)](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#dagi-v1-in-enlisted-2019), related_engine_gi)
- Stop light leaking through walls at grid lookups: Normal and camera offsets with clip blending; trade-off: there is no visibility term. ([Radiance grid](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#radiance-grid), related_engine_gi)
- Stop light passing through walls in 3D probe interpolation: Occlusion test during interpolation; trade-off: the test adds work per lookup. ([Positions on 3D RC](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#positions-on-3d-rc), history_and_landscape)

- Stop light leaks at the border of interpolation volumes: Samples pushed inward before filtering; trade-off: samples shifted off their true position. ([Convex offset filtering](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#convex-offset-filtering), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)

## Stop self-hits and rays escaping geometry

Start with [Rays starting inside geometry](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#rays-starting-inside-geometry) and the tracer side in [Robustness](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#robustness). The bias terms are explained in [Shadow maps, acne and bias](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#shadow-maps-acne-and-bias).

- Drop interval overlap by making the tracer watertight: Watertight tracing lets the bilinear fix skip overlap; trade-off is parallax fixes still need it. ([Overlap](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#overlap), cascade_layout_and_merging)
- Stop solids from ringing or darkening at cascade starts: Interior mask returns black above cascade 0; darkens at 512 squared. ([Rays starting inside geometry](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#rays-starting-inside-geometry), artifacts_and_fixes)
- Stop grazing rays tunnelling through thin occluders: Outward two-pixel field bias; occluders effectively grow by two pixels. ([Scene representations](../topics/2d_implementations.md#scene-representations), 2d_implementations)
- Stop probes firing rays from inside walls: Ray splitting spawns rays at visible surfaces; trade-off is atomic deposit cost. ([Ray splitting](../topics/3d_world_space.md#ray-splitting), 3d_world_space)
- Stop probes from self-hitting the surface they sit on: Pull-in toward the camera or normal offset; trade-off is light leaking at thin edges. ([Probe placement](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#probe-placement), 3d_screen_space)
- Keep probes that start inside solids from going black; Stop self-hits when tracing approximate distance fields: Push probes out or ignore back faces; rays may then pass through walls. ([Robustness](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#robustness), scene_representation_and_tracing)
- Stop screen-space rays passing through or hitting phantom walls: Per-pixel slab thickness decides hits; trade-off: too thin misses walls, too thick adds phantoms. ([Depth thickness](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#depth-thickness), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Stop rays slipping through shared triangle edges: Watertight shear test uses 2D edge functions; trade-off: extra per-ray setup work. ([Möller-Trumbore](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#möller-trumbore), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Stop surfaces from shadowing themselves in shadow maps; Stop RC rays from hitting the surface they start on: Depth bias and normal offset remove acne; trade-off: too much bias detaches shadows. ([Shadow maps, acne and bias](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#shadow-maps-acne-and-bias), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)

## Handle depth edges and upscaling

Start with [Merging across depth edges](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#merging-across-depth-edges), [Min/max probes](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#minmax-probes) and [Depth-aware upscaling](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#depth-aware-upscaling). The building blocks are [Hi-Z and min/max depth pyramids](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#hi-z-and-minmax-depth-pyramids) and the glossary's [depth-aware upscaling](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#depth-aware-upscaling).

- Cut surface GI cost by gathering at reduced resolution: Love and Deepspace gather at about 70 percent; trade-off is less gather detail. ([3D world space and surfaces](../topics/performance.md#3d-world-space-and-surfaces), performance)
- Cut 2D RC cost by tracing below output resolution: Quarter-resolution cascades; trade-off is cascades upscaled to output resolution. ([Community 2D timings](../topics/performance.md#community-2d-timings), performance)
- Upscale low-resolution GI without bleeding across depth edges: Choose between bilinear and best-depth taps, or use bilinear 3D; trade-off is extra weighting work. ([Depth-aware upscaling](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#depth-aware-upscaling), 3d_screen_space)
- Stop light and shadow leaking across depth edges in merges: Bilinear 3D weights follow the surface; trade-off is a few iterations and zero-length edge risk. ([Merging across depth edges](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#merging-across-depth-edges), 3d_screen_space)
- Remove depth-edge artifacts at tile boundaries in screen probes: Two probes per tile at min and max depth; costs twice the rays and memory. ([Min/max probes](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#minmax-probes), 3d_screen_space)
- Place min/max probes on exact depth pixels: Two-surface construction: a full-resolution bilateral filter finds discontinuities and min/max are computed only there; trade-off: costly on mobile. ([Min/max probes](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#minmax-probes), 3d_screen_space)
- Put extra probes where one depth per tile fails: Adaptive screen probes, the spatial counterpart of min/max probes in depth. ([Filling gaps and keeping anchors](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#filling-gaps-and-keeping-anchors), 3d_screen_space)
- Remove splotchy leaks at depth edges in screen-space probe RC: Min+Max probes; trade-off is 2 to 17% more time and twice the memory. ([Kaméus (2025): min/max probe placement](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#kaméus-2025-minmax-probe-placement), 3d_screen_space_theses)
- Upscale a low-resolution buffer to full resolution without depth halos: Bilinear 3D weights follow the surface; trade-off is no guard against zero-length edges. ([4XXSWS: depth-aware upscaling](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#4xxsws-depth-aware-upscaling), 3d_screen_space_implementations)
- Compute occlusion from prefiltered depth moments in closed form: Trapezoid antiderivative gives mean overlap exactly; trade-off is the uniform-offset assumption. ([l3scz8: occlusion from depth variance](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#l3scz8-occlusion-from-depth-variance), 3d_screen_space_implementations)
- Interpolate min/max depth without perspective error: Interpolate 1/d and world x, then blend in depth; trade-off is only approximately perspective-correct. ([NfBSz1: perspective-correct min/max interpolation](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#nfbsz1-perspective-correct-minmax-interpolation), 3d_screen_space_implementations)
- Upscale fog or volume buffers without bleeding over foreground edges: Rotated disc with a depth-spread gate; the disc's TAA purpose is gone in single-frame RC. ([pixelmager's depth-aware upscaling gist](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#pixelmagers-depth-aware-upscaling-gist), 3d_screen_space_implementations)
- Refill newly disoccluded regions with GI quickly: Temporal reservoirs and validation; newly disoccluded areas stay noisy until refilled. ([kajiya](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#kajiya), related_radiance_caches)
- Smooth noise without blurring depth or colour edges: Bilateral filter; trade-off: range weights need a full-resolution guide such as depth. ([Bilateral filter](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#bilateral-filter), supporting_techniques)
- Upscale probe or screen-space GI without bleeding across depth edges: Depth-aware upscaling; trade-off: needs a depth or normal guide at full resolution. ([Depth-aware upscaling](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#depth-aware-upscaling), supporting_techniques)
- Keep texture detail when rendering below display resolution: Negative mip bias log2(render/display) − 1; trade-off: sharper sampling can alias. ([Mip bias under upscaling](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#mip-bias-under-upscaling), supporting_techniques)
- Upscale jittered low-resolution frames with temporal history: Temporal Super Resolution; trade-off: convergence takes about 1000/(ScreenPercentage²·fps) ms. ([Temporal Super Resolution](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#temporal-super-resolution), supporting_techniques)
- Split a tile's depths into two layers: Lloyd two-centroid split; trade-off: fixed to exactly two layers. ([2-means clustering](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#2-means-clustering), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)

## Hide seams, gaps and blocky patches

Start with [Gaps and seams between cascades](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#gaps-and-seams-between-cascades) and [Grid and blocky penumbras](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#grid-and-blocky-penumbras). Interval overlap and interpolation that cause or hide seams are in [Intervals](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#intervals) and [Interpolation and band limits](../topics/core_theory.md#interpolation-and-band-limits).

- Hand off screen to world GI without hand-tuned lengths: State the hand-off as an RC interval boundary, its length in probe sizes with pull-back on the far read; every engine system measured picks its lengths by hand. ([Where the evidence stops](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#where-the-evidence-stops), single_frame_3d_rc)
- Remove blocky interpolation in dark areas: GradientRC slopes give higher-order storage; tested only on a 1/r point source. ([Interpolation and band limits](../topics/core_theory.md#interpolation-and-band-limits), core_theory)
- Keep 2D rays continuous across cascade levels: Forked layouts share start points; trade-off is lost angular resolution. ([Flatland](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#flatland), cascade_layout_and_merging)
- Tile each ray with contiguous intervals and no gaps: Contiguous geometric ×4 starts at zero; trade-off is interval lengths grow 4× per cascade. ([Intervals](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#intervals), cascade_layout_and_merging)
- Spread interpolation error evenly between misaligned 3D cascades: Half-cell offsets in 3D; trade-off is probe and direction offsets must match exactly. ([Probe placement](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#probe-placement), cascade_layout_and_merging)
- Remove blocky penumbras at shadow edges: 3x3 distance-weighted kernel removes blocks but averages across walls. ([Grid and blocky penumbras](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#grid-and-blocky-penumbras), artifacts_and_fixes)
- Avoid smeared transmittance when interpolating volumetric probes: Log-space extrapolation; trade-off: no visibility gradient, so tolerance must be lowered. ([Radiance caching in media (Jarosz et al. 2008)](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#radiance-caching-in-media-jarosz-et-al-2008), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Hide cascade seams with staggered interval ends: Staggered ends with fixed merge weights blur toward the lower neighbour. ([Bevy](../topics/2d_implementations.md#bevy), 2d_implementations)
- Keep penumbrae smooth when merging across probes: Four bilinear upper probes give smooth penumbrae; the author says Figure M's weights are wrong. ([Max Coppen, Fundamentals of Radiance Cascades](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#max-coppen-fundamentals-of-radiance-cascades), 2d_articles_and_demos)
- Blend probe values across cell boundaries smoothly: Corner gradient interpolation; trade-off: steps at every cell edge. ([Diagrams and helpers](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#diagrams-and-helpers), 2d_shadertoys)
- Remove haloes at cascade interval boundaries: Cross-faded interval ends; trade-off: the fade applies at every boundary. ([fad's line](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#fads-line), 2d_shadertoys)
- Hide LOD seams without an overlap zone: Axis-aligned cubic boundaries with 4× interval scaling; one build needed no blending. ([Placement and levels of detail](../topics/3d_world_space.md#placement-and-levels-of-detail), 3d_world_space)
- Hide the seams between probe levels of different spacing: LOD blending band crossfades levels; weights jump at integer LODs, unexplained in code. ([Probes and LOD](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#probes-and-lod), 3d_world_space_implementations)
- Remove interval-boundary bias in one frame without temporal history: Blended or overlapping interval boundaries and several rays per texel; block jitter does the same over frames. ([Connections](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding.md#connections), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding)
- Remove seams between records filled in one pass: Two passes, second with 1.8 times the error tolerance; costs some detail. ([Fixes from production](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#fixes-from-production), related_radiance_caches)
- Remove bilinear artifacts from cascade merging: Trace each lower probe to its four upper probes; trade-off: more merge work per probe. ([First public implementations and the Shadertoy lineage](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#first-public-implementations-and-the-shadertoy-lineage), history_and_landscape)
- Fill holes in sparse probe or sample images: Pull-push pyramid of coverage-weighted averages; trade-off: gaps come out blurred. ([Pull-push](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#pull-push), supporting_techniques)
- Pick probe detail by distance with fewer grid-alignment artifacts: Chebyshev-distance LOD steps per power of two; trade-off: coarse level changes. ([Chebyshev distance](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#chebyshev-distance), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Interpolate probes smoothly across position and direction: Pentalinear blends probes and directions together; trade-off: 32 taps per lookup. ([Multilinear interpolation](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#multilinear-interpolation), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)

## Avoid aliasing

Start with [Sky aliasing](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#sky-aliasing) and [Sharp shadows and small lights](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#sharp-shadows-and-small-lights). Sample patterns that spread error are in [Sampling and noise](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#sampling-and-noise).

- Keep a small sun from aliasing in the top cascade: Analytic sky integral per bin; no closed form in 3D. ([Sky and environment](../topics/core_theory.md#sky-and-environment), core_theory)
- Stop a small sun from aliasing in the top cascade: Integrate the sky per bin; more shader work than point sampling. ([Sky aliasing](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#sky-aliasing), artifacts_and_fixes)
- Stop aliasing from one-pixel lights: Integrating light over polygons instead of sampling; trade-off: no shadows yet. ([Polygonal cascades](../topics/holographic_rc.md#polygonal-cascades), holographic_rc)
- Add sky and sun to a 2D RC without aliasing: Sky-bin integral: a small sun does not alias; only top-level misses use it. ([Unity and other ports](../topics/2d_implementations.md#unity-and-other-ports), 2d_implementations)
- Stop sub-pixel texture detail flickering at distant hits: Mip 2 albedo sampling steadies distant hits at the cost of texture sharpness. ([Shading the hit](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#shading-the-hit), scene_representation_and_tracing)
- Rotate a fixed direction set uniformly at random each frame: Arvo's rotation from three uniform numbers; each frame needs its own draw. ([Arvo's random rotations](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#arvos-random-rotations), supporting_techniques)
- Dither sampling error into fine grain, not structure: Void-and-cluster threshold array; trade-off: a precomputed array to build and store. ([Blue noise and void-and-cluster](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#blue-noise-and-void-and-cluster), supporting_techniques)
- Generate extensible well-spread points per coordinate: Halton radical inverse; trade-off: one prime base per coordinate. ([Halton sequence](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#halton-sequence), supporting_techniques)
- Build a fixed N-point set with stratified first coordinate: Hammersley set; trade-off: the count N is fixed, so the set cannot be extended. ([Hammersley set](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#hammersley-set), supporting_techniques)
- Get temporally stable pixel noise without a texture fetch: Interleaved gradient noise; trade-off: pattern is designed for TAA across frames. ([Interleaved gradient noise](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#interleaved-gradient-noise), supporting_techniques)
- Converge sampled integrals faster than plain random sampling: Low-discrepancy point sets; trade-off: no error estimate without randomization. ([Low-discrepancy sequences](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#low-discrepancy-sequences), supporting_techniques)
- Keep texture detail when rendering below display resolution: Negative mip bias log2(render/display) − 1; trade-off: sharper sampling can alias. ([Mip bias under upscaling](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#mip-bias-under-upscaling), supporting_techniques)
- Spread jittered probe samples with one multiply-add per point: R2 recurrence; trade-off: two dimensions only, the 2D analogue of the golden-ratio sequence. ([R2](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#r2), supporting_techniques)
- Anti-alias after shading without temporal history: SMAA or FXAA post-process; trade-off: SMAA needs lookup textures, FXAA is one cheap pass. ([SMAA and FXAA](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#smaa-and-fxaa), supporting_techniques)
- Scramble digital sequences without losing stratification: Owen scrambling of Sobol points; trade-off: needs published direction-number tables. ([Sobol sequence and Owen scrambling](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#sobol-sequence-and-owen-scrambling), supporting_techniques)
- Spread ray directions evenly over a sphere for any count: Spherical Fibonacci points; trade-off: sets do not nest, so children cannot average into parents. ([Spherical Fibonacci points](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#spherical-fibonacci-points), supporting_techniques)
- Filter textures per sample without blending before shading: Stochastic texture filtering; trade-off: noise needs accumulation or a denoiser. ([Stochastic texture filtering and stochastic interpolation](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#stochastic-texture-filtering-and-stochastic-interpolation), supporting_techniques)
- Reduce aliasing by blending jittered frames over time: Temporal anti-aliasing with reprojection; trade-off: needs motion vectors and stale-history rejection. ([Temporal anti-aliasing and reprojection](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#temporal-anti-aliasing-and-reprojection), supporting_techniques)
- Place points on a disk without radial bands: Golden-angle spiral with radius c·√n; trade-off: defined for a disk, not a sphere. ([Vogel spiral](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#vogel-spiral), supporting_techniques)
- Avoid aliasing when sampling light or probes: Sample above twice the band limit; trade-off: denser probes cost memory and time. ([Nyquist-Shannon sampling](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#nyquist-shannon-sampling), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)

## Avoid banding and keep colour and precision right

Start with [Banding and precision](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#banding-and-precision) and [Colour space and range](../topics/core_theory.md#colour-space-and-range). Storage formats are in [Formats](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#formats) and the glossary's [Formats and colour](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#formats-and-colour).

- Keep RC lighting correct in linear colour: Linear colour pipeline; a brighter result shows ringing more clearly. ([Colour space and range](../topics/core_theory.md#colour-space-and-range), core_theory)
- Pick a final gather normalisation that matches the display: Mean radiance or fluence; trade-off is the fluence output is 2π brighter. ([Final gather](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#final-gather), cascade_layout_and_merging)
- Cut RC bandwidth by storing radiance in smaller formats: R11G11B10F and RG16F targets; trade-off is precision lost when merged values are re-stored. ([Formats](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#formats), cascade_layout_and_merging)
- Remove banding in dark scenes at 8-bit output: Animated dithering, with the least change in perceived brightness. ([Banding and precision](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#banding-and-precision), artifacts_and_fixes)
- Weight b-type rays in scenes with transparency: Distance-based b-type weights; trade-off: they fail in transparent scenes. ([Merging down](../topics/holographic_rc.md#merging-down), holographic_rc)
- Conserve energy when a light partly covers a direction: EnergyConservingHRC coverage rule; trade-off: no recorded results, one hard-coded light. ([Plane cascades](../topics/holographic_rc.md#plane-cascades), holographic_rc)
- Keep brightness constant across mip levels: Per-cascade mip marching; trade-off: one mip for every cascade drifts up to 30% brighter. ([Marching on mips](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#marching-on-mips), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Keep solid-angle normalization consistent across cascades: Pre-weighted solid-angle storage; trade-off: shading with 2π/n gave half the irradiance. ([RC for CT volumes (repository)](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#rc-for-ct-volumes-repository), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Turn RGB colours into spectra inside a renderer; Keep spectral throughput from underflowing in single precision: Sigmoid polynomial uplift; trade-off: 32-bit coefficient table that does not interpolate linearly. ([Spectral transport](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#spectral-transport), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Store transmittance precisely in volume textures: 16-bit volume textures; trade-off: RGBA16F cost 500 ms against RGBA8's 40 ms. ([Storage and colour](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#storage-and-colour), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Avoid clipping and banding in cascade storage: RGBA16F or R11G11B10F storage; RGBA8 clipping and banding is the failure. ([Connections across implementations](../topics/2d_implementations.md#connections-across-implementations), 2d_implementations)
- Average light correctly in a colour pattern: Average linear radiance before encoding; sRGB averages come out darker (128 vs 186). ([Shaders that are not RC](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#shaders-that-are-not-rc), 2d_shadertoys)
- Accumulate radiance without 64-bit atomics: Fixed-point u32 atomicAdd accumulators; trade-off: radiance clamped at 16 before storing. ([Data layout](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#data-layout), 3d_world_space_implementations)
- Stop NaN surfels and empty intervals from breaking surfel passes: Depth cutoff and grazing rejection; far and grazing pixels spawn no surfels. ([Artifacts and fixes](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#artifacts-and-fixes), 3d_surface_probes)
- Stop feedback bounces from gaining energy: Feedback needs decay or a reset, or closed boxes gain energy. ([Multi-bounce](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#multi-bounce), light_transport_alternatives)
- Keep sweep solutions from blurring light: Higher-order interval schemes cut defocusing; Hermitian is 1.46% off. ([Sweep solvers for radiative transfer](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#sweep-solvers-for-radiative-transfer), light_transport_alternatives)
- Give every direction texel the same solid angle: Equal-area octahedral map; trade-off: a more complex mapping than the plain octahedral. ([Equal-area octahedral map (Clarberg)](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#equal-area-octahedral-map-clarberg), supporting_techniques)
- Compute the solid angle of a spherical triangle exactly: Girard's theorem; trade-off: needs all three angles. ([Girard's theorem](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#girards-theorem), supporting_techniques)
- Integrate sphere functions exactly up to a given polynomial degree: Lebedev quadrature; trade-off: point sets exist only for fixed degrees. ([Lebedev quadrature](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#lebedev-quadrature), supporting_techniques)
- Hide 8-bit banding in dark gradients: Animated dither offset before 8-bit output; trade-off: a moving per-pixel offset in every channel. ([Valve's animated dither](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#valves-animated-dither), supporting_techniques)
- Clamp history colour with less chroma error: YCoCg; trade-off: per-pixel transform; only the lifting form is exact on integers. ([YCoCg](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#ycocg), supporting_techniques)
- Keep transmittance precise over very thin segments: expm1 avoids cancellation near zero; trade-off: helps only when the argument is small. ([expm1](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#expm1), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Pick the right radiometric quantity for RC output: Fluence versus mean radiance differs by 2π in 2D; trade-off: normalization must match the consumer. ([Radiometric quantities](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#radiometric-quantities), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Keep depth precision even for distant geometry: Reversed Z in float depth balances precision; trade-off: needs clip control set in OpenGL. ([Reversed Z](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#reversed-z), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Filter colour in linear light, not encoded values: Decode to linear before filtering; trade-off: extra decode and encode steps. ([sRGB](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#srgb), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Map HDR radiance to screen range without washing out colour: Curves such as Reinhard, ACES and AgX; trade-off: each shifts hue and contrast differently. ([Tone mapping](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#tone-mapping), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
