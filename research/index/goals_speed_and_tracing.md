## Trace rays without hardware ray tracing

Start with [What RC asks of a tracer](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#what-rc-asks-of-a-tracer) and [Options](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#options), then [Voxels](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs.md#voxels), [Triangle BVHs](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs.md#triangle-bvhs) and [Software against hardware](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs.md#software-against-hardware). Traversal algorithms are in the glossary under [Ray traversal and intersection](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#ray-traversal-and-intersection).

- Trace rays in a Godot compute shader without hardware RT: Compute tracer with segment-length structures; trade-off is unknown throughput, floor 0.375 G segments/s. ([Tracing and scene representation under `RenderingDevice`](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#tracing-and-scene-representation-under-renderingdevice), single_frame_3d_rc)
- Trace screen-space GI in about 1 ms: PoE2's depth-buffer march; trade-off is a top-down camera and no temporal reuse. ([3D screen space](../topics/performance.md#3d-screen-space), performance)
- Measure world-space 3D RC inside Godot's RenderingDevice: gomezzz's software BVH tracer; trade-off is a CPU BVH build of about 1.22 s. ([Godot](../topics/performance.md#godot), performance)
- Estimate a Godot compute tracer's ray rate: Compute BVH or voxel DDA, 1 to 3 G rays per second; trade-off is speed. ([Normalized throughput (derived)](../topics/performance.md#normalized-throughput-derived), performance)
- Measure a RenderingDevice compute tracer on RC rays; Test whether hardware-RT results hold with a compute tracer: Compute BVH or voxel DDA timed per pass; trade-off is only a frame-capped floor exists. ([Open questions](../topics/performance.md#open-questions), performance)
- Trace cascade −1 in 3D against the scene representation: Screen-space march as in Split RC; trade-off is unknown leak behaviour at contact. ([Open questions](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#open-questions), cascade_layout_and_merging)
- Trace rays through a 2D scene quickly: Occupancy pyramid skips empty cells, but the pyramid must be built. ([Scene representations](../topics/2d_implementations.md#scene-representations), 2d_implementations)
- Light a 2D scene without probes or cascades: Random rays per pixel with jump flood field; accumulates over frames, no timings. ([GM Shaders (Yaazarai and Xor)](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders.md#gm-shaders-yaazarai-and-xor), 2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders)
- Trace rays without hardware ray tracing: Software segment DDA or quantized BVH; trade-off: about 3.5× slower than hardware RT. ([Tracing for world-space cascades](../topics/3d_world_space.md#tracing-for-world-space-cascades), 3d_world_space)
- Run Split RC on a RenderingDevice without hardware ray tracing: Native wroughtinator build: one GLSL compute source, software BVH, no hardware ray tracing. ([Split RC codes](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#split-rc-codes), 3d_world_space_implementations)
- Build a dense world probe grid from an SDF scene: Sphere-trace each probe into an octahedral atlas; trade-off: full rebuilds, no per-frame update. ([Passes](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations_grids.md#passes), 3d_world_space_implementations_grids)
- Trace rays through a voxel world with large steps: Distance-field stepping with DDA near surfaces; trade-off: per-chunk field storage. ([rbnelr/voxel_game](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations_grids.md#rbnelrvoxel_game), 3d_world_space_implementations_grids)
- Trace thin objects in a depth march without leaks: Per-sector masks or moment prefiltering; trade-off is extra storage and merge complexity. ([Depth-buffer marching](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#depth-buffer-marching), 3d_screen_space)
- Pick the fastest compute tracer for short SPWI rays: Compare BVH2, wide BVH, voxels and SDF under RenderingDevice; open which is fastest. ([Open questions](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#open-questions), 3d_screen_space)
- Trace rays without hardware ray tracing: Software BVH on compute; a binary BVH is faster for coherent RC rays. ([World rays](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#world-rays), 3d_screen_space)
- Trace SPWI intervals through a voxel grid: 256³ voxel grid with 400 samples per interval; trade-off is upper cascades skip voxels. ([KingTheFifth/radiance-cascades](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#kingthefifthradiance-cascades), 3d_screen_space_implementations)
- Trace rays against scene triangles without hardware ray tracing: Software BVH on compute; tracing is the main cost, 5.35 ms in the README profile. ([Gathering (`gather.slang`)](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#gathering-gatherslang), 3d_surface_probes)
- Trace rays against meshes on phones without triangle BVHs: Ray-disk tests against per-mesh surfels, cheaper than triangles; surfels approximate geometry. ([Scene representation](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#scene-representation), 3d_surface_probes)
- Trace a sparse distance field in compute without hardware RT: Brixelizer's field is approximate and softens hard shadows as voxels grow. ([Brixelizer](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#brixelizer), scene_representation_and_tracing)
- Match each cascade's tracer to its ray length: Fine exact structure for low cascades, coarse mips or BVH for long intervals; inferred. ([Connections](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#connections), scene_representation_and_tracing)
- Remove the pixelated zero contour from an unsigned distance field; Build a scene distance field each frame from seeds quickly: Signed field at 0.5 threshold keeps edges smooth; distances past 8 px saturate. ([Jump flooding](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#jump-flooding), scene_representation_and_tracing)
- Trace screen-space rays without skipping or double-sampling pixels: Perspective-correct DDA visits each pixel once; zThickness must match object thickness. ([McGuire and Mara's perspective-correct DDA](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#mcguire-and-maras-perspective-correct-dda), scene_representation_and_tracing)
- Pick a BVH layout for coherent cascade rays: BVH2 traversal beat wider layouts in tinybvh tests; incoherent rays were untested. ([BVH width and ray coherence](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs.md#bvh-width-and-ray-coherence), scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs)
- Stream a sparse voxel octree on demand from ray requests: Ray requests fetch bricks as needed; a 128 MB cache gives 5.9% misses. ([GigaVoxels: sparse voxel octrees with ray-guided streaming](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs.md#gigavoxels-sparse-voxel-octrees-with-ray-guided-streaming), scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs)
- Trace voxel cascades in Godot with 32-bit integer operations only: Integer walk with 8 fractional bits needs no 64-bit atomics. ([HDDAGI's voxel cascades](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs.md#hddagis-voxel-cascades), scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs)
- Rebuild a CPU BVH quickly enough when meshes move: gomezzz build takes about 1.2 s for 72,012 triangles, and moving meshes reset history. ([Other software BVHs in RC projects](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs.md#other-software-bvhs-in-rc-projects), scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs)
- Decide when software triangle tracing is fast enough for RC: Software ran about 3.5 times slower than hardware RT; grids beat BVHs on short rays. ([Software against hardware](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs.md#software-against-hardware), scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs)
- Skip empty voxel blocks in a DDA without distance fields: Occupancy at every level gives exact first hits, at the cost of storing it. ([The hierarchical DDA family](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs.md#the-hierarchical-dda-family), scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs)
- Trace triangle BVHs in GLSL compute without hardware ray tracing: Start with tiny.comp's two-wide layout and shared-memory stack, adding segment limits and any-hit exits. ([tinybvh](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs.md#tinybvh), scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs)
- Voxelize Godot meshes without geometry shaders or mesh RIDs: Per-axis raster passes or triangle compute; skinned meshes need their own path. ([Voxelization in Godot](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs.md#voxelization-in-godot), scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs)
- Rebuild a triangle BVH every frame on the GPU: Morton-sorted LBVH in four compute passes, no refit; build cost grows with triangle count. ([Wicked Engine: a BVH built on the GPU every frame](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs.md#wicked-engine-a-bvh-built-on-the-gpu-every-frame), scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs)
- Trace rays without a distance field: Hierarchical DDA over voxel bit masks; trade-off: probe interpolation leaks at corners. ([HDDAGI](../topics/godot_integration.md#hddagi), godot_integration)
- Voxelize scenes without a geometry shader: Three-axis orthographic voxelization; trade-off: three passes, and only static meshes contribute. ([SDFGI, the shipped GI](../topics/godot_integration.md#sdfgi-the-shipped-gi), godot_integration)
- Gather indirect light with a few cones per pixel: Voxel cone tracing: prefiltered mips per cone; blurs the scene and leaks through thin walls. ([Voxel cone tracing](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#voxel-cone-tracing), related_probe_gi)
- Run GI without hardware ray tracing: Compute-only sparse SDF with screen probes; trade-off: light is missing until surfaces reappear on screen. ([Brixelizer GI](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#brixelizer-gi), related_engine_gi)
- Trace GI rays without hardware ray tracing: SDF clipmap march with lit-voxel hits; trade-off: lit voxels leak more than the SDF. ([daGI2](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#dagi2), related_engine_gi)
- Voxelize closed meshes without a BVH: Inside-outside voxelization by ray parity; open meshes give noisy results. ([Berhard's sweep framework](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#berhards-sweep-framework), light_transport_alternatives)
- Trace rays on the GPU without hardware ray tracing: Persistent threads with while-while BVH loops; trade-off: traversal runs on general shader cores. ([Aila and Laine traversal](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#aila-and-laine-traversal), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Reuse one mesh across many transformed instances: BLAS per geometry, TLAS of instances; trade-off: instance transforms add top-level cost. ([BLAS and TLAS](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#blas-and-tlas), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Trace rays against many triangles without testing each one; Build BVHs on the GPU fast enough for per-frame use: BVH subtree skipping; trade-off: build time and memory for traversal speed. ([BVH](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#bvh), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Fill every voxel a thin triangle touches: Conservative rasterization overestimates coverage; trade-off: fattens geometry and wastes cells. ([Conservative rasterization](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#conservative-rasterization), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Walk rays through a voxel grid cell by cell: Per-axis tMax and tDelta stepping visits cells in order; trade-off: long rays take more steps. ([DDA grid traversal](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#dda-grid-traversal), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Compute distance fields on a grid without a heap: Gauss-Seidel fast sweeping is O(N); trade-off: sweep count grows as 2^n in n dimensions. ([Eikonal equation and fast sweeping](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#eikonal-equation-and-fast-sweeping), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Build distance fields from triangle meshes on the GPU: DiFi rasterizes per-primitive distances; trade-off: cost grows with primitives and grid slices. ([GPU distance fields from meshes](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#gpu-distance-fields-from-meshes), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Build an approximate distance field in a few GPU passes: Jump flooding in log2 n passes; trade-off: approximate, with extra passes to cut errors. ([Jump flooding](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#jump-flooding), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Test rays against triangles with little stored per-triangle data: Möller-Trumbore needs no precomputed data; trade-off: slower than Baldwin-Weber's transform. ([Möller-Trumbore](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#möller-trumbore), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Trace hardware-accelerated rays from any shader stage: Ray query loops run in compute; trade-off: not exposed through Godot's RenderingDevice. ([Ray queries](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#ray-queries), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Test ray hits against boxes without branches: Slab test with IEEE infinities; trade-off: depends on IEEE float edge behaviour. ([Slab test](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#slab-test), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Ray cast voxel scenes without storing empty space: Sparse voxel octree keeps only occupied octants; trade-off: pointer-heavy traversal. ([Sparse voxel octrees](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#sparse-voxel-octrees), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- March rays through a distance field without overshooting surfaces: Sphere tracing steps by field value; trade-off: needs a Lipschitz bound on the field. ([Sphere tracing](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#sphere-tracing), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)

## Skip empty space and trace long rays cheaply

Start with [Marching on mips](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#marching-on-mips), [The hierarchical DDA family](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs.md#the-hierarchical-dda-family) and [Distance fields](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#distance-fields). Line sweeping avoids marching altogether: [Line sweeping](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#line-sweeping).

- Trace 8 to 12 M rays in 4 ms: HDDA over bitmask bricks with mip marching; trade-off is throughput not yet verified. ([Open problems, ranked](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#open-problems-ranked), single_frame_3d_rc)
- Extend a short ray march into a full interval: Extend identity doubles marches; exact on regular grids, approximated by vanilla merging. ([Shift and extend](../topics/core_theory.md#shift-and-extend), core_theory)
- Trace long rays without marching their full length: Extensions build long rays from short ones; trade-off: stored transmittance and bent rays. ([Building rays: the acceleration structure](../topics/holographic_rc.md#building-rays-the-acceleration-structure), holographic_rc)
- Skip empty blocks during interval tracing without fixed steps: Hierarchical DDA over packed block states; trade-off: only 1.5× gain on a half-empty model. ([DexRT](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#dexrt), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Keep brightness constant across mip levels; Skip empty voxel blocks in tracing without leaks; Pick an averaging rule that fits media density; Stop marching early once a ray is nearly opaque: Per-cascade mip marching; trade-off: one mip for every cascade drifts up to 30% brighter. ([Marching on mips](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#marching-on-mips), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Choose per block between density and coverage averaging: Jensen-gap bound on structure orientation; trade-off: not yet tested. ([Open questions](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#open-questions), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Skip unchanged blocks in a 2D scene: Per-block 64-bit masks let a pixel DDA skip unchanged 8×8 blocks. ([CPU references and research harnesses](../topics/2d_implementations.md#cpu-references-and-research-harnesses), 2d_implementations)
- Trace rays through a 2D scene quickly: Occupancy pyramid skips empty cells, but the pyramid must be built. ([Scene representations](../topics/2d_implementations.md#scene-representations), 2d_implementations)
- Cut rays in 2D scenes with empty or solid areas: Sparse probe tasks cut rays 4–30×; interpolation is not built. ([Single-frame assessment](../topics/2d_implementations.md#single-frame-assessment), 2d_implementations)
- Afford interval scaling l = 4 with a marching tracer: Grow step length with interval using filtered proxies; trade-off: per-sample mip handling. ([Marching cost](../topics/3d_world_space.md#marching-cost), 3d_world_space)
- Cut the cost of long rays through a voxel world: Clipmap LOD grows voxel size with distance; trade-off: blur from larger voxels far away. ([creikey/voxel-radiance-cascades](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations_grids.md#creikeyvoxel-radiance-cascades), 3d_world_space_implementations_grids)
- Trace rays through a voxel world with large steps: Distance-field stepping with DDA near surfaces; trade-off: per-chunk field storage. ([rbnelr/voxel_game](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations_grids.md#rbnelrvoxel_game), 3d_world_space_implementations_grids)
- Cut the ray count of a dense cascade 0 grid: Probe culling of empty cells; trade-off: per-frame voxelization, which the paper calls not core. ([Mapping and culling](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#mapping-and-culling), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg)
- Trace only probes with visible geometry each frame: Live probe culling via occupancy mips; trade-off: the scene is voxelized every frame. ([Tauray code](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#tauray-code), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg)
- Match each cascade's tracer to its ray length: Fine exact structure for low cascades, coarse mips or BVH for long intervals; inferred. ([Connections](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#connections), scene_representation_and_tracing)
- Trace far cascades from coarse prefiltered scene data: Per-cascade mip caps give speed; coarse prefiltered data leaks through thin walls. ([What RC asks of a tracer](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#what-rc-asks-of-a-tracer), scene_representation_and_tracing)
- Pick a BVH layout for coherent cascade rays: BVH2 traversal beat wider layouts in tinybvh tests; incoherent rays were untested. ([BVH width and ray coherence](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs.md#bvh-width-and-ray-coherence), scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs)
- Skip empty voxel blocks in a DDA without distance fields: Occupancy at every level gives exact first hits, at the cost of storing it. ([The hierarchical DDA family](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs.md#the-hierarchical-dda-family), scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs)
- Propagate light per pixel with cellular automata: Cone sweeping is O(1) per pixel, but few stored angles leak. ([Cellular automata and cone sweeping](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#cellular-automata-and-cone-sweeping), light_transport_alternatives)
- Trace long rays cheaply on a uniform screen grid: Line sweeps: O(1) per pixel per direction; volumetric intervals need extra storage. ([Line sweeping](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#line-sweeping), light_transport_alternatives)
- Skip empty screen regions when tracing depth: Hi-Z pyramid lets rays jump whole regions; trade-off: pyramid build pass and extra memory. ([Hi-Z and min/max depth pyramids](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#hi-z-and-minmax-depth-pyramids), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Skip empty space when ray-marching sparse volumes: Hierarchical DDA steps over whole empty nodes; trade-off: needs a VDB-style node tree. ([Hierarchical DDA](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#hierarchical-dda), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Choose the filter level that matches a cone's footprint: Cell size near 2r(t) sets filter width; trade-off: no closed form through voxel mips. ([Cone footprint](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#cone-footprint), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Regroup divergent ray work for coherence: Regroups rays across threads; trade-off: vendor-specific and not exposed through Godot. ([Shader execution reordering](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#shader-execution-reordering), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)

## Cut ray counts and reuse work

Start with [Rays traced and rays built](../topics/core_theory.md#rays-traced-and-rays-built) and [Cascade scaling](../topics/core_theory.md#cascade-scaling), then [Ray splitting](../topics/3d_world_space.md#ray-splitting) and [Reservoir Cascades](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades.md).

- Cut surface GI cost by gathering at reduced resolution: Love and Deepspace gather at about 70 percent; trade-off is less gather detail. ([3D world space and surfaces](../topics/performance.md#3d-world-space-and-surfaces), performance)
- Cut 2D RC cost by tracing below output resolution: Quarter-resolution cascades; trade-off is cascades upscaled to output resolution. ([Community 2D timings](../topics/performance.md#community-2d-timings), performance)
- Meet the angular requirement without more storage: Pre-average many rays per stored cone; stored texel count stays the same. ([Rays cast against directions stored](../topics/core_theory.md#rays-cast-against-directions-stored), core_theory)
- Skip upper-cascade rays where neighbouring probes agree: Adaptive skipping; trade-off is visible artefacts at the aggressive 40 percent mode. ([Merge kernels](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#merge-kernels), cascade_layout_and_merging)
- Measure whether pre-averaging costs quality in 3D: Pre-averaging with farther-apart children; trade-off is storage saved against accuracy. ([Open questions](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#open-questions), cascade_layout_and_merging)
- Halve HRC cost by dropping probe rows: Keeps one lattice and interpolates; trade-off: half the transverse probes. ([Half HRC](../topics/holographic_rc.md#half-hrc), holographic_rc)
- Reuse in-scattered light across nearby medium points; Keep multiple scattering affordable in dense media: Gradient-sized records with log-space extrapolation; trade-off: queries take a third to half of render time. ([Radiance caching in media (Jarosz et al. 2008)](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#radiance-caching-in-media-jarosz-et-al-2008), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Trace only the probes that matter in sparse 2D scenes: Sparse cascades cut rays; interpolation is not built and slabs cost memory. ([Bevy](../topics/2d_implementations.md#bevy), 2d_implementations)
- Cut upper-cascade rays in 2D RC: Adaptive ray skipping: 10–40% fewer rays for 36–68 dB PSNR. ([Unity and other ports](../topics/2d_implementations.md#unity-and-other-ports), 2d_implementations)
- Avoid casting every direction through every cascade: Branch factor 4 merges four upper intervals into one; ringing is left out of scope. ([Max Coppen, Fundamentals of Radiance Cascades](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#max-coppen-fundamentals-of-radiance-cascades), 2d_articles_and_demos)
- Keep per-pixel cost bounded while upper cascades resolve far lights: Two-pass split: 32 raymarches per pixel reach 256 directions; cost is unmeasured. ([Single-frame assessment](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#single-frame-assessment), 2d_articles_and_demos)
- Share ray work between neighbouring pixels: Jump flood field; trade-off: log2(max(W,H)) flood passes per field build. ([Part 1: naive GI](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_jason_today.md#part-1-naive-gi), 2d_articles_and_demos_jason_today)
- Reach 256 ray directions per pixel at 32 raymarches: Packed two-pass gives 256 directions for 32 raymarches; one sample per group leaves a grid. ([Part 2: building RC](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_jason_today.md#part-2-building-rc), 2d_articles_and_demos_jason_today)
- Remove ringing with one trace per ray: Dolkar mask, if its bracket matches the merge; bilinear fix costs 16 traces per texel. ([Open questions](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#open-questions), 2d_shadertoys)
- Cut the cost of ray-traced AO by reusing cached hits: Cache per-cell AO hit ratios in a spatial hash; trade-off: static scenes only. ([Spatial hashing for ray-traced AO](../topics/3d_world_space.md#spatial-hashing-for-ray-traced-ao), 3d_world_space)
- Share ray cost across directions for SPWI as SSRC does: Sector casting or marching world structures along screen lines; open whether it beats ray tracing. ([Open questions](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#open-questions), 3d_screen_space)
- Bound reuse bias across all cascade levels; Keep reuse variance from compounding across cascade depth: Sum of ε_n stays under 3·ε0 at any depth, given bounded radiance. ([Theory](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades.md#theory), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades)
- Cut the ray count of a dense cascade 0 grid: Probe culling of empty cells; trade-off: per-frame voxelization, which the paper calls not core. ([Mapping and culling](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#mapping-and-culling), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg)
- Shade multi-bounce paths without a second trace: Multi-bounce shading from last frame's cascade 0; trade-off: colour lost and visible block edges. ([Ray casting and gathering](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#ray-casting-and-gathering), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg)
- Trace only probes with visible geometry each frame: Live probe culling via occupancy mips; trade-off: the scene is voxelized every frame. ([Tauray code](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#tauray-code), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg)
