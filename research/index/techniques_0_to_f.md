## Numbers and symbols

- **0.25/0.5/0.25 phase filter**: Filter against phases that alternate along the probe axis. [holographic_rc: Final gather and cleanup](../topics/holographic_rc.md#final-gather-and-cleanup). Also in [holographic_rc_implementations](../topics/holographic_rc_implementations.md#jasontodayhrc).
- **2-means clustering**: Lloyd's two-centroid clustering; in 1D a threshold at the midpoint of the means. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: 2-means clustering](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#2-means-clustering).
- **6×6 octahedral irradiance tile**: Final gather tile per probe, from Split RC and erhe. ## A

- **ACES fit**: Narkowicz's rational fit to the ACES curve, clamped to unit range. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Tone mapping](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#tone-mapping). Also in [2d_shadertoys](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#media), [3d_world_space_implementations_grids](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations_grids.md#m3ycwt-mathis-voxel-cascades-with-hemisphere-probes).
- **AgX**: Sobotka's log-encoded, matrix-wrapped S-curve transform from Blender. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Tone mapping](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#tone-mapping).
- **Aila and Laine traversal**: Persistent threads pulling rays from a global pool, using while-while loops. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Aila and Laine traversal](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#aila-and-laine-traversal).
- **Aliasing law for small lights**: A light spanning x ray gaps errs by sqrt(f(1-f))/x RMS, at most 1/(2x); 10% takes five gaps; in 3D the one-ray-per-bin error falls as about x^−0.68, measured. [core_theory: Choosing cascade 0 (derived and reported)](../topics/core_theory.md#choosing-cascade-0-derived-and-reported). Also in [artifacts_and_fixes](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#sharp-shadows-and-small-lights), [single_frame_3d_rc](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#where-the-evidence-stops).
- **Ambient cube**: Six values, one per axis, weighted by n² toward the normal's sign. [supporting_techniques: Ambient cube](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#ambient-cube). Also in [related_probe_gi](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#irradiance-volumes).
- **Ambient term**: Progressive radiosity's display correction: the remaining light spread evenly and reflected at the mean albedo. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Ambient term](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#ambient-term).
- **Analytic sky integral**: Sky(a0, a1) integrates sky brightness over a bin in closed form. [core_theory: Sky and environment](../topics/core_theory.md#sky-and-environment). Also in [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#who-built-what).
- **Anderson acceleration**: Speeds fixed-point iteration by mixing recent iterates to minimize the combined residual. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Anderson acceleration](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#anderson-acceleration). Also in [light_transport_alternatives](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#multi-bounce), [3d_world_space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#multi-bounce-specular-and-comparisons).
- **AR(1)**: Autoregressive model, X_t = φX_{t−1} + ε_t, with autocorrelation φ^k at lag k. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: AR(1)](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#ar1).
- **Arvo's random rotations**: Householder reflection composed with a z-rotation gives a uniform random rotation. [supporting_techniques: Arvo's random rotations](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#arvos-random-rotations).
- **Axis-aligned HRC**: Turns probes toward each ray; cut tracing 100 to 10 ms but blocks extensions. [holographic_rc: Equal-angle, ring, radial and axis-aligned HRC](../topics/holographic_rc.md#equal-angle-ring-radial-and-axis-aligned-hrc).

## B

- **Balance heuristic**: w_i = n_i·p_i / Σ_j n_j·p_j. [supporting_techniques: Multiple importance sampling](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#multiple-importance-sampling).
- **Balance model of the cascade 0 interval**: E^2 = A*t0/t_eq + B*t_eq/t0; optimum t_eq*sqrt(B/A), where angular and spatial error cross. [core_theory: Choosing cascade 0 (derived and reported)](../topics/core_theory.md#choosing-cascade-0-derived-and-reported).
- **Baldwin-Weber**: Per-triangle transform to barycentric space, trading memory for a faster test. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Möller-Trumbore](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#möller-trumbore).
- **Beer-Lambert law**: Transmittance T = exp(−∫σ_t ds) through an absorbing or scattering medium. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Beer-Lambert law](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#beer-lambert-law). Also in [core_theory](../topics/core_theory.md#radiance-intervals).
- **Bent normals and bent cones**: Mean unoccluded hemisphere direction, used in place of the normal for ambient lookups. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Bent normals and bent cones](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#bent-normals-and-bent-cones), [related_engine_gi: The 2021 final gather](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#the-2021-final-gather). Also in [related_screen_space_gi](../topics/related_screen_space_gi.md#visibility-bitmask-ssgi).
- **Beurling-Selberg functions**: Band-limited majorants or minorants of sgn or interval indicators, with minimal integral error. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Beurling-Selberg functions](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#beurling-selberg-functions).
- **Bilateral filter**: Weighted average by spatial distance and value difference, so edges survive. [supporting_techniques: Bilateral filter](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#bilateral-filter).
- **Bilateral upsampling**: Called one of the worst methods; unnormalized weights give dark edges and leaks. [3d_screen_space: Depth-aware upscaling](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#depth-aware-upscaling), [related_engine_gi: Mobile path](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#mobile-path).
- **Bilinear 3D merge**: Solves the bilinear ratio where upper world points interpolate the lower point; no normalization. [3d_screen_space: Merging across depth edges](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#merging-across-depth-edges), [3d_screen_space_theses: Gavras (2025): SPWI on Metal](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#gavras-2025-spwi-on-metal).
- **Bilinear fix**: Traces one ray per upper probe, merges each, then blends the four bilinearly. [artifacts_and_fixes: The bilinear fix](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#the-bilinear-fix), [cascade_layout_and_merging: Merge kernels](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#merge-kernels), [2d_implementations: CPU references and research harnesses](../topics/2d_implementations.md#cpu-references-and-research-harnesses), [2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders: GM Shaders (Yaazarai and Xor)](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders.md#gm-shaders-yaazarai-and-xor). Also in [2d_articles_and_demos](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#max-coppen-fundamentals-of-radiance-cascades), [2d_shadertoys](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#the-testbed-line), [light_transport_alternatives](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#caustics-and-refraction), [3d_screen_space](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#measurements), [3d_screen_space_implementations](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#scy3dd-spwi-in-one-shader), [3d_world_space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#leak-defences-in-world-space), [3d_world_space_implementations_grids](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations_grids.md#rbnelrvoxel_game), [3d_world_space_implementations](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#merge-modes).
- **Bilinear interpolation**: Four-tap linear interpolation in two dimensions. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Multilinear interpolation](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#multilinear-interpolation).
- **Bilinear weights**: Specified weights 0.25 and 0.75; GM Shaders' smoother 0.33 weights. [cascade_layout_and_merging: Merge kernels](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#merge-kernels), [2d_articles_and_demos: Otavio Peixoto, OPEngine](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#otavio-peixoto-opengine).
- **Binary BVH for coherent RC rays**: Reported 2× faster than an 8-wide compressed BVH for SPWI. [3d_screen_space: World rays](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#world-rays), [scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs: BVH width and ray coherence](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs.md#bvh-width-and-ray-coherence).
- **Binned SAH**: Scores only K−1 boundaries of K centroid bins; K=16 near optimal, 4 reasonable. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: BVH](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#bvh).
- **BLAS and TLAS**: Bottom-level structure per geometry and top-level structure of instances in hardware ray tracing. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: BLAS and TLAS](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#blas-and-tlas).
- **Blelloch tree scan**: Tree-form exclusive scan doing O(n) work in O(log n) steps. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Prefix sums and decoupled look-back](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#prefix-sums-and-decoupled-look-back).
- **Block cascades**: Each probe is an N×N block indexing lines by angle and offset. [holographic_rc: Block cascades](../topics/holographic_rc.md#block-cascades).
- **Block-consistent interval jitter**: Interval boundaries scaled by a log-uniform factor redrawn every K frames. [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades: Method](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades.md#method). Also in [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding.md#connections).
- **Blocky interpolation patches**: Blocks in dark areas from probe resolution; survive every merge fix. [core_theory: Interpolation and band limits](../topics/core_theory.md#interpolation-and-band-limits), [artifacts_and_fixes: Grid and blocky penumbras](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#grid-and-blocky-penumbras).
- **Blue noise and void-and-cluster**: Little low-frequency energy, so error looks like fine, even grain. [supporting_techniques: Blue noise and void-and-cluster](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#blue-noise-and-void-and-cluster).
- **BMFR**: Blockwise multi-order feature regression, solved by augmented QR with stochastic regularization. [supporting_techniques: BMFR](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#bmfr). Also in [3d_surface_probes](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#love-and-deepspace).
- **Bounce feedback**: Last frame's fluence times albedo over 2π is added back as emission. [holographic_rc_implementations: cute_framework and folkjs](../topics/holographic_rc_implementations.md#cute_framework-and-folkjs), [2d_implementations: Connections across implementations](../topics/2d_implementations.md#connections-across-implementations), [2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders: GM Shaders (Yaazarai and Xor)](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders.md#gm-shaders-yaazarai-and-xor), [godot_integration: HDDAGI](../topics/godot_integration.md#hddagi). Also in [2d_articles_and_demos](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#single-frame-assessment), [light_transport_alternatives](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#multi-bounce).
- **Branching factor**: General scaling: spacing, directions and interval growth set by alpha or K and l. [core_theory: Cascade scaling](../topics/core_theory.md#cascade-scaling). Also in [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#explainers-and-guides).
- **Bresenham's line algorithm**: Integer error term steps a line one pixel per step, with no multiply or divide. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Bresenham's line algorithm](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#bresenhams-line-algorithm).
- **Bucketed cuckoo hashing**: Three-function bucketed cuckoo reaching load factors near 0.98. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: GPU hash tables](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#gpu-hash-tables).
- **BVH**: Tree of bounding volumes over primitives; rays skip any subtree whose box they miss. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: BVH](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#bvh). Also in [holographic_rc](../topics/holographic_rc.md#measured-results-and-the-cost-model).

## C

- **C_MINUS1_GATHERING**: Traces the first interval [0, L0] per pixel, in front of interpolated cascade 0. [2d_shadertoys: The testbed line](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#the-testbed-line). Also in [cascade_layout_and_merging](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#cascade-1-and-contact-detail), [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#first-public-implementations-and-the-shadertoy-lineage).
- **Cascade 0**: Finest RC cascade, whose probes trace whole spheres at one angular resolution. [related_probe_gi: Connections](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#connections). Also in [related_radiance_caches](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#connections).
- **Cascade count**: Enough cascades that the top interval passes the farthest distance a ray must reach. [cascade_layout_and_merging: Cascade count](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#cascade-count), [2d_implementations: TypeGPU (Software Mansion)](../topics/2d_implementations.md#typegpu-software-mansion), [2d_articles_and_demos: Otavio Peixoto, OPEngine](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#otavio-peixoto-opengine), [2d_articles_and_demos_jason_today: Part 2: building RC](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_jason_today.md#part-2-building-rc).
- **Cascade −1**: Per-pixel short trace from 0 to the cascade 0 start, merged before four probes. [cascade_layout_and_merging: Cascade −1 and contact detail](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#cascade-1-and-contact-detail), [3d_world_space_implementations: Merge, history and shading](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#merge-history-and-shading), [3d_screen_space: Path of Exile 2](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#path-of-exile-2), [3d_screen_space: Final gather](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#final-gather). Also in [2d_implementations](../topics/2d_implementations.md#setups-that-worked-in-2d), [single_frame_3d_rc](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#where-existing-designs-lean-on-time), [related_screen_space_gi](../topics/related_screen_space_gi.md#visibility-bitmask-ssgi), [3d_world_space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#samples-per-direction-per-frame-derived), [artifacts_and_fixes](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#cheaper-fixes), [related_radiance_caches](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#connections), [3d_surface_probes: Measured against a reference](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#measured-against-a-reference).
- **Chebyshev distance**: L-infinity distance, the largest of the absolute x, y and z differences. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Chebyshev distance](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#chebyshev-distance).
- **Chebyshev LOD rule**: Probe level of detail from the binary log of Chebyshev distance to the camera. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Chebyshev distance](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#chebyshev-distance).
- **Chebyshev test**: Bounds visibility from mean and squared distance moments; the test DDGI also uses. [related_probe_gi: Light-field probes](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#light-field-probes). Also in [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding.md#connections).
- **Clipmaps and toroidal addressing**: Nested fixed-size windows with modulo addressing, so moving windows rewrite only exposed strips. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Clipmaps and toroidal addressing](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#clipmaps-and-toroidal-addressing). Also in [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#positions-on-3d-rc).
- **Coefficient of variation**: Unitless relative spread, σ over μ, for ratio-scale data. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Coefficient of variation](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#coefficient-of-variation).
- **Composite (`composite.slang`)**: Final gather of up to four cascade 0 surfels, times albedo, times 4 pi. [3d_surface_probes: Composite (`composite.slang`)](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#composite-compositeslang).
- **Compute BVH**: Compute-shader BVH at 2.8 to 3.9 G rays per second on current GPUs. [performance: Normalized throughput (derived)](../topics/performance.md#normalized-throughput-derived). Also in [single_frame_3d_rc](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#budgets), [3d_surface_probes](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#a-generated-surface-atlas-blackline-interactive).
- **Cone footprint**: Cone of half-angle θ has radius t·tanθ, sampled at the matching cell level. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Cone footprint](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#cone-footprint).
- **Cone merge**: Merges all child directions of a parent to approximate the parent's cone. [core_theory: Angle: cones, not rays](../topics/core_theory.md#angle-cones-not-rays). Also in [3d_world_space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#merge-and-interpolation).
- **Cone sweeping**: 45° cone endpoints move one pixel per step; O(1) per pixel. [light_transport_alternatives: Cellular automata and cone sweeping](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#cellular-automata-and-cone-sweeping). Also in [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#timeline).
- **Cone tracing**: Trades noise for blocky, aliased shadows; one ray per cone adds aliasing. [3d_world_space: Tracing for world-space cascades](../topics/3d_world_space.md#tracing-for-world-space-cascades). Also in [3d_world_space_implementations_grids](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations_grids.md#creikeyvoxel-radiance-cascades).
- **Cone-tracing mips**: Uses extensions as mips for cone tracing in vanilla RC. [holographic_rc: Building rays: the acceleration structure](../topics/holographic_rc.md#building-rays-the-acceleration-structure). Also in [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#ray-extensions-with-media).
- **Conservative rasterization**: Rasterizes every pixel a triangle touches, or only the pixels it fully covers. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Conservative rasterization](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#conservative-rasterization). Also in [3d_surface_probes: Building a surface atlas](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#building-a-surface-atlas).
- **Constant cost**: RC's high fixed cost: a 3D cascade 0 costs like voxelizing the scene. [core_theory: Constant cost](../topics/core_theory.md#constant-cost). Also in [related_screen_space_gi](../topics/related_screen_space_gi.md#connections).
- **Convex offset filtering**: Pushes border samples into the convex volume before filtering. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Convex offset filtering](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#convex-offset-filtering), [related_engine_gi: daGI v1 in Enlisted (2019)](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#dagi-v1-in-enlisted-2019).
- **Cosine-weighted diffuse gather**: Cosine-weighted integral of cascade 0 against the normal, giving diffuse light. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Lambertian BRDF](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#lambertian-brdf).
- **Cranley-Patterson rotation**: Adds one random vector modulo 1 to every point; averaged shifts are unbiased. [supporting_techniques: Cranley-Patterson rotation](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#cranley-patterson-rotation).
- **Cross-faded interval ends**: Fades hits near cascade boundaries so the two weights sum to one. [artifacts_and_fixes: Cheaper fixes](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#cheaper-fixes), [2d_shadertoys: fad's line](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#fads-line).
- **Cube maps and face subdivision**: Six square 90° faces; a direction picks the face of its largest component. [supporting_techniques: Cube maps and face subdivision](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#cube-maps-and-face-subdivision), [cascade_layout_and_merging: Direction maps in 3D](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#direction-maps-in-3d). Also in [performance](../topics/performance.md#volumetric-and-hrc).
- **Cuckoo hashing**: Gives each key several candidate slots and evicts on insert, bounding lookup probes. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: GPU hash tables](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#gpu-hash-tables).
- **CWBVH**: Compressed wide BVH with 8-bit child planes; 1.9–2.1× faster incoherent traversal, 35–60% memory. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: BVH](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#bvh). Also in [scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs: GPU builds and wide nodes, measured](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing_voxels_and_bvhs.md#gpu-builds-and-wide-nodes-measured).

## D

- **DDA grid traversal**: Steps a ray cell by cell, always crossing the nearest boundary via tMax and tDelta. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: DDA grid traversal](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#dda-grid-traversal), [performance: Normalized throughput (derived)](../topics/performance.md#normalized-throughput-derived), [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer: Integration and merging](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#integration-and-merging), [2d_articles_and_demos: tmpvar's playground](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#tmpvars-playground). Also in [single_frame_3d_rc](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#budgets), [3d_world_space_implementations_grids](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations_grids.md#m3ycwt-mathis-voxel-cascades-with-hemisphere-probes).
- **DDGI**: Dynamic probe grid: each probe traces a full sphere per frame into irradiance maps. [related_probe_gi: DDGI](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#ddgi). Also in [3d_world_space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#leak-defences-in-world-space), [3d_world_space_implementations](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#reduce-into-the-probe-field), [cascade_layout_and_merging](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#direction-maps-in-3d), [performance](../topics/performance.md#what-the-numbers-show), [godot_integration](../topics/godot_integration.md#the-sdfgi-plan), [related_engine_gi](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#mobile-path), [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#non-english-overviews), [related_radiance_caches](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#gibs-and-pica-pica-surfels).
- **DDGI resampling**: Resampled DDGI at 21.81 ms over 1.33 frames at equal error. [related_probe_gi: DDGI resampling](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#ddgi-resampling). Also in [performance](../topics/performance.md#related-techniques).
- **Decay with age**: Weight and radiance decay by 0.9 past weight 3 or after 12 frames. [3d_world_space: Temporal reuse in world space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#temporal-reuse-in-world-space), [3d_world_space_implementations: Unreal world-space Split RC port](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#unreal-world-space-split-rc-port).
- **Delta tracking**: Samples collisions against a majorant, accepting real ones with probability σ_t/σ_maj. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Delta tracking](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#delta-tracking). Also in [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#marching-on-mips).
- **Dense world grid**: Probes on a regular grid, every cascade stored, world-locked. [single_frame_3d_rc: Dense and clipmap world grids](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#dense-and-clipmap-world-grids), [performance: Budgets for single-frame 3D RC (derived)](../topics/performance.md#budgets-for-single-frame-3d-rc-derived).
- **Depth-aware upscaling**: Joint bilateral upsampling guided by depth and often normals. [3d_screen_space: Depth-aware upscaling](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#depth-aware-upscaling), [supporting_techniques: Depth-aware upscaling](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#depth-aware-upscaling). Also in [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#path-of-exile-1-shadows-and-gi).
- **Depth bias**: Constant or slope-scaled offset subtracted before the depth comparison. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Shadow maps, acne and bias](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#shadow-maps-acne-and-bias).
- **Depth-buffer marching**: Marches a single depth layer; needs no scene representation but has unknown thickness. [3d_screen_space: Depth-buffer marching](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#depth-buffer-marching).
- **Depth reprojection**: Reads each parent toward the hit depth estimated from the parents' stored depths; exact for planes, keeps about 3.7(a/h)² of a light narrower than half the parent spacing. [cascade_layout_and_merging: Merge kernels](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#merge-kernels). Also in [core_theory](../topics/core_theory.md#parallax-in-bin-units-derived), [artifacts_and_fixes: Fixes compared](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#fixes-compared), [source note](../sources/2026_kurushimee_merging_3d.md#reading-toward-a-depth-derived).
- **Depth thickness**: Assumes each depth pixel is a slab of thickness behind the stored depth. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Depth thickness](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#depth-thickness).
- **Diagonal rule**: Cascade 0 interval at least the cell diagonal: sqrt(2) spacings in 2D. [core_theory: Choosing cascade 0 (derived and reported)](../topics/core_theory.md#choosing-cascade-0-derived-and-reported). Also in [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#derived-points).
- **Diagonal taps**: Four diagonal taps at 2^(i-1) texels per pass, cancelling 2, 4 and 8 px patterns. [holographic_rc: Final gather and cleanup](../topics/holographic_rc.md#final-gather-and-cleanup). Also in [holographic_rc_implementations](../topics/holographic_rc_implementations.md#pixi-gi).
- **DiFi**: Rasterized per-primitive distance functions that build mesh distance fields on the GPU. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: GPU distance fields from meshes](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#gpu-distance-fields-from-meshes).
- **Dilation (lightmaps)**: Uncovered border texels copy a covered neighbour so bilinear reads skip empty texels. [3d_surface_probes: Building a surface atlas](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#building-a-surface-atlas).
- **Direction-first layout**: One direction holds every probe; merge takes one filtered read with pre-averaging. [cascade_layout_and_merging: Position-first and direction-first](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#position-first-and-direction-first), [2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders: GM Shaders (Yaazarai and Xor)](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders.md#gm-shaders-yaazarai-and-xor), [3d_screen_space_theses: Gavras (2025): SPWI on Metal](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#gavras-2025-spwi-on-metal), [3d_screen_space_theses: Dell'Ova (2025): performance scaling with DXR](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#dellova-2025-performance-scaling-with-dxr). Also in [2d_articles_and_demos](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#max-coppen-fundamentals-of-radiance-cascades), [3d_surface_probes](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#x3xfrm-mathis), [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#explainers-and-guides), [related_probe_gi](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#connections).
- **Distance field**: Seeds flooded in log2 passes to store each pixel's nearest-seed distance. [2d_articles_and_demos: radiance-cascades.com demo and Osborne's slide demo](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#radiance-cascadescom-demo-and-osbornes-slide-demo), [2d_articles_and_demos_jason_today: Part 1: naive GI](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_jason_today.md#part-1-naive-gi). Also in [performance](../topics/performance.md#normalized-throughput-derived).
- **DLSS Ray Reconstruction**: NVIDIA's DLSS 3.5 neural denoiser that replaces hand-tuned ray-tracing denoisers; version 4.5 is Preset F. [supporting_techniques: DLSS Ray Reconstruction](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#dlss-ray-reconstruction). Also in [DLSS Ray Reconstruction 4.5](../sources/2026_dlss_ray_reconstruction_4_5.md).
- **Down-sweep**: Combines segments from the level above into fluence, averaging halves on even x. [holographic_rc_implementations: Down-sweep: fluence](../topics/holographic_rc_implementations.md#down-sweep-fluence).

## E

- **Edge-avoiding à-trous wavelet filter**: B3-spline passes, tap spacing doubling each pass, with edge-stopping weights. [supporting_techniques: Edge-avoiding à-trous wavelet filter](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#edge-avoiding-à-trous-wavelet-filter), [godot_projects: Phreakyx: voxel-cone RC as a GDExtension](../topics/godot_projects.md#phreakyx-voxel-cone-rc-as-a-gdextension). Also in [godot_integration](../topics/godot_integration.md#screen-probes-in-122999).
- **Eikonal equation and fast sweeping**: Solves |∇u| = f by upwind Gauss-Seidel sweeps in alternating orders. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Eikonal equation and fast sweeping](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#eikonal-equation-and-fast-sweeping).
- **EnvBRDFApprox**: Analytic fit of the BRDF scale and bias, replacing the table on mobile. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Split-sum approximation](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#split-sum-approximation).
- **Equal-angle HRC**: Gives each frustum equal width in radians; removes HRC's crosses, 1.05 ms on RTX 5090. [holographic_rc: Equal-angle, ring, radial and axis-aligned HRC](../topics/holographic_rc.md#equal-angle-ring-radial-and-axis-aligned-hrc). Also in [holographic_rc_implementations](../topics/holographic_rc_implementations.md#nihil).
- **Equal-area map**: Maps R2 points onto the sphere with equal-area cells; turned against axis-aligned walls, it avoids an error jump near the equal-error t0 in 3D. [3d_world_space: Ray splitting](../topics/3d_world_space.md#ray-splitting). Also in [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#mapping-and-culling), [cascade_layout_and_merging](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#direction-maps-in-3d).
- **Equal-area octahedral map (Clarberg)**: Constant-Jacobian square-to-sphere map; each 2×2 child block is a quarter of its parent. [supporting_techniques: Equal-area octahedral map (Clarberg)](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#equal-area-octahedral-map-clarberg), [cascade_layout_and_merging: Direction maps in 3D](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#direction-maps-in-3d), [3d_screen_space_theses: Dell'Ova (2025): performance scaling with DXR](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#dellova-2025-performance-scaling-with-dxr). Also in [3d_screen_space](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#directions-in-3d).
- **Equal-error rule**: Sets t0 = spacing over angular step; 1.60 spacings at 32 directions in 3D, measured as vanilla's best t0 in 3D rooms. [core_theory: Choosing cascade 0 (derived and reported)](../topics/core_theory.md#choosing-cascade-0-derived-and-reported). Also in [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#derived-points).
- **Error decomposition of RC**: Exact split into angular radiance, angular transmittance, correlation, spatial (seams and parallax) and gather terms. [Source note: error model](../sources/2026_kurushimee_rc_error_model.md#error-decomposition). Also in [artifacts_and_fixes: Gaps and seams between cascades](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#gaps-and-seams-between-cascades).
- **Even-probe averaging**: Averages two estimates at even x to avoid bias toward cone centres. [holographic_rc: Merging down](../topics/holographic_rc.md#merging-down). Also in [holographic_rc_implementations](../topics/holographic_rc_implementations.md#pixi-gi).
- **Exact flatland reference**: Interval radiance and transmittance as exact step functions of angle, by clipping segments to an annulus. [Source note: error model](../sources/2026_kurushimee_rc_error_model.md#reference-and-setup). Also in [core_theory: Choosing cascade 0 (derived and reported)](../topics/core_theory.md#choosing-cascade-0-derived-and-reported).
- **expm1**: Computes e^x − 1 without cancellation near zero, keeping thin-segment transmittance precise. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: expm1](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#expm1).
- **Exponential moving average (α = 1/30)**: Averages each texel's jittered ray samples over frames; delays updates. [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg: Cascades](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#cascades). Also in [related_probe_gi](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#light-field-probes).
- **Extend identity**: Doubles an interval by reading itself at shifted positions, O(1) per doubling. [core_theory: Shift and extend](../topics/core_theory.md#shift-and-extend). Also in [single_frame_3d_rc](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#where-the-evidence-stops).
- **Extended gathering**: Gathers many directions per pixel from one live cascade, with no merge. [2d_shadertoys: Extended gathering](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#extended-gathering), [light_transport_alternatives: Extended gathering](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#extended-gathering).
- **Extension from cascade 0**: Cheaper start for extensions; leaves crosses around small emitters and caps angular resolution. [holographic_rc: Building rays: the acceleration structure](../topics/holographic_rc.md#building-rays-the-acceleration-structure), [holographic_rc_implementations: Volumetric-HRC](../topics/holographic_rc_implementations.md#volumetric-hrc).

## F

- **Fast marching**: Finalizes grid nodes in order of arrival time, using a heap. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Eikonal equation and fast sweeping](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#eikonal-equation-and-fast-sweeping).
- **Fast multipole method**: Evaluates pairwise 1/r interactions in O(N) with multipole and local expansions. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Fast multipole method](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#fast-multipole-method).
- **FFT convolution**: Convolution as spectral multiplication, zero-padded to avoid wrap-around, O(N log N). [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: FFT convolution](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#fft-convolution).
- **Filament energy scaling**: Scales specular by 1 + F0(1/r − 1) from the DFG table. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Multiple-scattering energy compensation](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#multiple-scattering-energy-compensation).
- **Final gather**: Each pixel blends the four nearest cascade 0 probes and sums their cones. [cascade_layout_and_merging: Final gather](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#final-gather), [holographic_rc: Final gather and cleanup](../topics/holographic_rc.md#final-gather-and-cleanup), [3d_screen_space: Final gather](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#final-gather), [3d_screen_space_theses: Gavras (2025): SPWI on Metal](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#gavras-2025-spwi-on-metal). Also in [3d_surface_probes](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#x3xfrm-mathis).
- **Fireflies**: Failure mode: small high-contribution regions make RCPG's MSE worse than PT's. [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg: Evaluation](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#evaluation). Also in [related_probe_gi](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#ddgi), [related_radiance_caches](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#gibs-and-pica-pica-surfels).
- **Fixed-point atomic accumulation**: Sums scaled to integers and accumulated with integer atomicAdd, so order-independent. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Float atomics](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#float-atomics).
- **FLIP**: Perception-based full-reference metric mapping the difference a viewer sees between two images. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: FLIP](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#flip). Also in [light_transport_alternatives](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#caustics-and-refraction), [3d_surface_probes](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#on-surface-radiance-caches-tatzgern-et-al-2024), [3d_world_space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#multi-bounce-specular-and-comparisons), [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#evaluation).
- **Float atomics**: Atomic float add and exchange on buffers and images; Godot shaders cannot rely on them. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Float atomics](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#float-atomics).
- **Fluence**: Radiance integrated over all directions without cosine; HRC works in fluence. [core_theory: Radiometry in flatland](../topics/core_theory.md#radiometry-in-flatland), [holographic_rc: Geometry](../topics/holographic_rc.md#geometry), [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Radiometric quantities](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#radiometric-quantities).
- **Fluence versus mean radiance**: 2D RC codes disagree on output quantity, a 2π brightness factor. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Radiometric quantities](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#radiometric-quantities).
- **Forked layouts**: Child rays start from one shared point at the parent's centre angle. [cascade_layout_and_merging: Flatland](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#flatland). Also in [performance](../topics/performance.md#what-the-numbers-show).
- **Forking**: Every child ray group starts at one shared parent-end point; paths connect. [artifacts_and_fixes: Cheaper fixes](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#cheaper-fixes). Also in [core_theory](../topics/core_theory.md#where-the-merge-is-exact-derived).
- **Forking fix**: Moves each ray's start; 4clcWn draws it switchable. [2d_shadertoys: Diagrams and helpers](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#diagrams-and-helpers). Also in [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#community-site-wiki-and-tools).
- **Formal solution**: Without scattering, I(s) = I(s0)e^(−τ) plus emission along the path. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Radiative transfer equation and discrete ordinates](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#radiative-transfer-equation-and-discrete-ordinates).
- **Frame order**: Fixed per-frame pass order; spawn reads last frame's hash grid, merge runs coarse to fine. [3d_surface_probes: Frame order](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#frame-order).
- **Front parents**: A surface probe's merge keeps only the upper parents in front of its tangent plane, renormalized; removes a 9.5 to 20% dark bias of bent merges on world grids. [3d_surface_probes: Measured against a reference](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#measured-against-a-reference).
- **Froxels**: View-aligned frustum voxel grid, small near the camera, for injecting and integrating media. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Froxels](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#froxels). Also in [3d_world_space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#placement-and-levels-of-detail), [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#single-frame-assessment).
- **Frozen solve**: An RC solve with stored hit records, re-merged with no rays to apply further bounces. [core_theory: Bounces](../topics/core_theory.md#bounces). Also in [light_transport_alternatives](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#multi-bounce), [single_frame_3d_rc](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#bounces).
