## Light many lights and emissive surfaces

Start with [Independence from light count and scene content](../topics/core_theory.md#independence-from-light-count-and-scene-content), then [Cascades and light sampling](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding.md#cascades-and-light-sampling). Light sampling methods are in the glossary under [Resampling and sampling theory](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#resampling-and-sampling-theory).

- Keep frame cost flat as lights increase: Fixed ray set per frame; cost still grows with how far rays march. ([Independence from light count and scene content](../topics/core_theory.md#independence-from-light-count-and-scene-content), core_theory)
- Add shadows to polygonal cascades: Split polygons at occluders or estimate partial coverage; trade-off: no shadows yet. ([Open questions](../topics/holographic_rc.md#open-questions), holographic_rc)
- Keep lighting cost flat as light count rises in volumes: Field precomputed per scene change; trade-off: no light-count sweep was measured. ([RC for medical volumes (Rulkens)](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#rc-for-medical-volumes-rulkens), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Compute 2D direct light exactly without sampling: Exact clipped visibility with sky integral; trade-off: segments cannot intersect or have thickness. ([Diagrams and helpers](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#diagrams-and-helpers), 2d_shadertoys)
- Light many dynamic lights in a URP game: RC adds about 1.2 to 1.8 ms; trade-off is daylight over-brightening and close noise. ([Gerassimov (2026): RC against Unity's GI](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#gerassimov-2026-rc-against-unitys-gi), 3d_screen_space_theses)
- Keep emissive triangles looked up correctly in complex scenes: A TLAS and material lookup are future work; the current lookup order breaks on Bistro. ([three-rc](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#three-rc), 3d_screen_space_implementations)
- Sample small bright lights without putting them in cascade texels: Explicit NEE outside cascades; RCPG sun in texels loses to NEE at fewer samples. ([Cascades and light sampling](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding.md#cascades-and-light-sampling), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding)
- Sample polygonal lights exactly inside a cascade texel: Solid-angle sampling is exact per texel; RCPG found it 25% slower for 0.0005 SSIM gain. ([Methods compared and cited](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding.md#methods-compared-and-cited), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding)
- Light small point and directional lights without probe cascades: NEE for small lights; trade-off: sun inside cascades leaves sunlit edges slow to converge. ([Ray casting and gathering](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#ray-casting-and-gathering), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg)
- Keep many local lights cheap in surfel GI: Stochastic lightcuts or 4 to 8 reservoir candidates; reservoir reuse across surfels unfinished. ([GIBS and PICA PICA surfels](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#gibs-and-pica-pica-surfels), related_radiance_caches)
- Sample many emissive lights cheaply; Direct light sampling with cascade cones: Light tree with O(log N) sampling; discussion-sourced build under 0.5 ms. ([Light sampling and guiding](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#light-sampling-and-guiding), light_transport_alternatives)
- Get soft shadows with natural penumbras at constant cost: Equal memory per cascade; trade-off: nothing is cached, so every frame converges from scratch. ([From 2D tests to the paper (2022 to 2023)](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#from-2d-tests-to-the-paper-2022-to-2023), history_and_landscape)
- Keep shadow cost flat as the light count grows: Shadow cascades cap each point's contributions; trade-off: needs PoE-specific engine properties. ([Path of Exile 1: shadows and GI](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#path-of-exile-1-shadows-and-gi), history_and_landscape)
- Sample many emitters with one walk down a light tree: Light tree with stochastic lightcuts; trade-off: correlated error becomes uncorrelated noise. ([Light trees and stochastic lightcuts](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#light-trees-and-stochastic-lightcuts), supporting_techniques)
- Sample direct light efficiently with shadow rays: Next-event estimation; trade-off: must combine with BSDF sampling by MIS. ([Next-event estimation](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#next-event-estimation), supporting_techniques)
- Pick good light candidates from a cheap distribution: Resampled importance sampling; trade-off: candidate weights need the target density. ([Resampled importance sampling (RIS)](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#resampled-importance-sampling-ris), supporting_techniques)
- Reuse light samples across pixels and frames for many lights: ReSTIR's reservoir merging; trade-off: needs the temporal history single-frame RC gives up. ([ReSTIR](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#restir), supporting_techniques)
- Shade many lights without re-rasterizing geometry: One geometry pass stores attributes; trade-off: memory bandwidth for each stored attribute. ([G-buffer and deferred shading](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#g-buffer-and-deferred-shading), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Shade polygonal area lights with a closed-form integral: Fitted matrices map lobes to clamped cosines; trade-off: fitted tables per view angle and roughness. ([Linearly transformed cosines](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#linearly-transformed-cosines), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)

## Guide and sample with cascades

Start with [RC for sampling and guiding](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding.md), then [Reservoir Cascades](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades.md) and [RCPG](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md). Background is in the glossary under [Resampling and sampling theory](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#resampling-and-sampling-theory).

- Guide path tracing with a distribution learned from paths: Cascade descent picks directions; deposits train it, but no lights are sampled. ([vlam](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding.md#vlam), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding)
- Lift Reservoir Cascades from 2D to 3D: Use J = r_q²/r_p² and direction-texture bins; shadow rays would dominate the 3D budget. ([Limits and design axes](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades.md#limits-and-design-axes), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades)
- Keep temporal reuse from favouring stale bright samples; Stop per-level weight products from growing across cascade levels: Re-evaluate stored samples each frame; without it, response to turn-off was zero after 48 frames. ([Method](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades.md#method), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_reservoir_cascades)
- Keep direction lookups faithful for specular and guiding: Direction-fidelity criterion; trade-off: standard RC's 2^n intervals violate it. ([Derived points](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#derived-points), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg)
- Keep directions of incoming light correct for guiding: Parallax-based interval bound; trade-off: the conservative bound wastes probe resolution. ([Parallax-based interval bounds](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#parallax-based-interval-bounds), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg)
- Guide path-traced bounces toward bright incoming light in real time: Cascade volume guides bounce sampling, not final lighting; trade-off: noise remains, plus extra traced rays. ([RCPG: product path guiding with radiance cascades](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#rcpg-product-path-guiding-with-radiance-cascades), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg)
- Sample radiance times BSDF from probes without bias; Keep in-texel samples above the horizon at grazing angles: Separability approximation with matching PDF weights; trade-off: underestimates grazing texels. ([Sampling](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#sampling), rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg)
- Keep ReSTIR neighbour reuse from overflowing: Jacobian rejection above 2; overflow still happens when left running for minutes. ([Bevy Solari](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#bevy-solari), related_radiance_caches)
- Trace probe rays where light arrives most: Ray guiding: CDF over reprojected cell luminance picks directions. ([GI-1.0](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#gi-10), related_radiance_caches)
- Shade indirect light with few rays: ReSTIR GI with spatial reuse; trade-off: denoising costs more than the tracing. ([PC and console path](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#pc-and-console-path), related_engine_gi)
- Direct light sampling with cascade cones: RC-guided NEE: brightest cone, an untested proposal from discussion. ([Light sampling and guiding](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#light-sampling-and-guiding), light_transport_alternatives)
- Estimate integrals with fewer samples where the integrand is large: Importance sampling with density p resembling f; trade-off: p must be positive wherever f is. ([Importance sampling](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#importance-sampling), supporting_techniques)
- Sample many emitters with one walk down a light tree: Light tree with stochastic lightcuts; trade-off: correlated error becomes uncorrelated noise. ([Light trees and stochastic lightcuts](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#light-trees-and-stochastic-lightcuts), supporting_techniques)
- Combine two sampling strategies without double counting: Multiple importance sampling; trade-off: weights need every technique's density. ([Multiple importance sampling](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#multiple-importance-sampling), supporting_techniques)
- Sample indirect directions from learned incident radiance: Path guiding with an SD-tree; trade-off: a learned tree that must be refined. ([Path guiding](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#path-guiding), supporting_techniques)
- Break correlation between neighbours in temporal reuse: Permutation sampling; trade-off: reads history at a permuted pixel offset. ([Permutation sampling](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#permutation-sampling), supporting_techniques)
- Pick good light candidates from a cheap distribution: Resampled importance sampling; trade-off: candidate weights need the target density. ([Resampled importance sampling (RIS)](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#resampled-importance-sampling-ris), supporting_techniques)
- Reuse light samples across pixels and frames for many lights: ReSTIR's reservoir merging; trade-off: needs the temporal history single-frame RC gives up. ([ReSTIR](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#restir), supporting_techniques)
- Pick one item from a weighted stream in constant memory: Weighted reservoir sampling; trade-off: keeps only one pick and the weight sum. ([Weighted reservoir sampling](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#weighted-reservoir-sampling), supporting_techniques)

## Use cascades and line space beyond lighting

Start with [Other uses](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#other-uses), [Solutions without occlusion](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#solutions-without-occlusion) and [Wave optics](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#wave-optics).

- Store only occupied blocks of line space: Blocks of line-indexed texels built from 2x2 children; trade-off: unweighted sums, unfinished. ([Block cascades](../topics/holographic_rc.md#block-cascades), holographic_rc)
- Resolve point-light falloff exactly in line space: Diagonal basis with probes √2 px apart; trade-off: occlusion remains open. ([Line space](../topics/holographic_rc.md#line-space), holographic_rc)
- Solve steady-state diffusion with a block hierarchy: Block elimination solves diffusion in 3 to 4 ms at 129²; trade-off: edge sampling unverified. ([Other uses](../topics/holographic_rc.md#other-uses), holographic_rc)
- Simulate long-range gravity with cascaded levels: Distance-band levels plus previous-frame merge; trade-off: far fields arrive over frames. ([Cascades for gravity](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#cascades-for-gravity), 2d_shadertoys)
- Cut the cost of ray-traced AO by reusing cached hits: Cache per-cell AO hit ratios in a spatial hash; trade-off: static scenes only. ([Spatial hashing for ray-traced AO](../topics/3d_world_space.md#spatial-hashing-for-ray-traced-ao), 3d_world_space)
- Compute ambient occlusion from cascaded horizons in one frame: Max-merged horizons approximate GTAO with two slices; trade-off is noiseless but artifact-prone output. ([33ycDm: GTAO from cascaded horizons](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#33ycdm-gtao-from-cascaded-horizons), 3d_screen_space_implementations)
- Add screen-space AO or GI as a compositor effect: Compositor passes read depth and normals; trade-off: they edit lit colour, so albedo is missing. ([Compositor effect projects](../topics/godot_projects.md#compositor-effect-projects), godot_projects)
- Cut per-pixel marching cost in screen-space AO: LSAO shares occluders along a sweep line; the cost stops depending on radius. ([Connections](../topics/related_screen_space_gi.md#connections), related_screen_space_gi)
- Add near-field bounce to screen-space AO with one buffer: Reprojected last-frame radiance gives near-field bounce; off-screen light and geometry are lost. ([Horizon-based indirect lighting](../topics/related_screen_space_gi.md#horizon-based-indirect-lighting), related_screen_space_gi)
- Compute ambient occlusion at any radius for a fixed cost: Line sweeps with an occluder stack; cost is banding and blur from shared directions. ([Line-sweep ambient obscurance](../topics/related_screen_space_gi.md#line-sweep-ambient-obscurance), related_screen_space_gi)
- Get AO and one-bounce indirect light from one per-slice bitmask: Bitmask gives AO and one-bounce GI; constant thickness leaks at depth steps. ([Visibility-bitmask SSGI](../topics/related_screen_space_gi.md#visibility-bitmask-ssgi), related_screen_space_gi)
- Compute sweep ambient occlusion from a depth map: LSAO: O(1) per ray with infinite thickness on height maps. ([Berhard's sweep framework](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#berhards-sweep-framework), light_transport_alternatives)
- Answer pathfinding connectivity with cascades: Cascade pathfinding: N³ build, log N queries, versus N⁴ for A*. ([Other uses](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#other-uses), light_transport_alternatives)
- Compute unoccluded 2D GI exactly with transforms; Reconstruct binary shapes from few-angle sinograms: 1/r convolution by FFT; cannot take internal barriers. ([Solutions without occlusion](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#solutions-without-occlusion), light_transport_alternatives)
- Propagate a wave field through obstacles row by row; Rebuild a sampled line field as plane waves: Plane-wave rebuild per row; the last row fills after about as many frames as rows. ([Wave optics](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#wave-optics), light_transport_alternatives)
- Draw grass and fur at constant cost per frame: Precomputed table makes cost independent of blade count; trade-off: precomputation takes seconds to 6 hours. ([Other work on Sannikov's channel](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#other-work-on-sannikovs-channel), history_and_landscape)
- Get directional ambient light from screen-space occlusion: Bent normal replaces the shading normal for ambient lookups; trade-off: one direction loses detail. ([Bent normals and bent cones](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#bent-normals-and-bent-cones), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Draw lattice lines exactly with integer arithmetic: Integer error term steps lines exactly; trade-off: suits lattice-aligned lines, not arbitrary geometry. ([Bresenham's line algorithm](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#bresenhams-line-algorithm), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Compute cosine-weighted screen-space occlusion in closed form: Slice horizon integral is closed form; trade-off: thin occluders need a thickness heuristic. ([GTAO](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#gtao), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Approximate hemisphere occlusion from the depth heightfield: Horizon marching over depth as a heightfield; trade-off: blind to geometry hidden behind depth. ([HBAO](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#hbao), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Compute ambient obscurance at large radii cheaply: Mip-pyramid depth keeps cost flat with radius; trade-off: approximate horizons from reduced depth. ([SAO](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#sao), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Add screen-space occlusion and indirect light without extra geometry: SSAO, HBAO, GTAO and SSGI read the depth buffer; trade-off: only on-screen surfaces count. ([Visibility, shadows and screen space](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#visibility-shadows-and-screen-space), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Compute long-range pairwise interactions without quadratic cost: Far clusters use multipole expansions; trade-off: accuracy set by tolerance, plus expansion setup. ([Fast multipole method](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#fast-multipole-method), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Convolve large kernels in O(N log N) time: Spectral multiplication after zero padding; trade-off: padding enlarges the transform. ([FFT convolution](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#fft-convolution), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Integrate over intervals with an exact endpoint node: Fixed endpoint node; trade-off: one degree less exact than Gauss with the same node count. ([Gauss-Radau quadrature](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#gauss-radau-quadrature), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Solve linear systems with fully parallel updates: Jacobi updates use only the last iterate; trade-off: needs spectral radius below 1. ([Jacobi iteration](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#jacobi-iteration), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Solve diffusion-like fields on a grid quickly: Relaxation sweeps on a grid; trade-off: many iterations until the field converges. ([Laplace diffusion](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#laplace-diffusion), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Find lines or structures by voting in line space: Hough voting in (ρ, θ) finds lines; trade-off: accumulator quantization and cost. ([Radon and Hough transforms, projection-slice theorem](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#radon-and-hough-transforms-projection-slice-theorem), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)

## Turn cascades into diffuse shading

Start with [Diffuse](../topics/core_theory.md#diffuse) and [Final gather](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#final-gather), then [Final gather](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#final-gather) in screen space and [Lighting the hits and the composite](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#lighting-the-hits-and-the-composite).

- Compute diffuse irradiance from RC cheaply: Merge everything into cascade 0; needs cosine weighting per cone. ([Diffuse](../topics/core_theory.md#diffuse), core_theory)
- Turn cascade 0 cones into per-normal irradiance cheaply; Pick a final gather normalisation that matches the display: 6×6 irradiance tiles per probe; trade-off is a precompute and store per probe. ([Final gather](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#final-gather), cascade_layout_and_merging)
- Choose irradiance tile size and placement for single-frame shading: Per-probe 6×6 tiles; trade-off is the unknown cost of per-pixel-group tiles. ([Open questions](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#open-questions), cascade_layout_and_merging)
- Make CT surface shading match exact irradiance: Projected solid-angle cone weights; trade-off: a precomputed per-cone table. ([Open questions](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#open-questions), volumetric_and_radiative_transfer)
- Cut the shading cost of cascade 0 probes: 6×6 octahedral map read at up to 8 probes per pixel; trade-off: fixed angular resolution. ([Shading](../topics/3d_world_space.md#shading), 3d_world_space)
- Feed RC into an existing DDGI probe path: Reduces cascade 0 into DDGI-format maps; trade-off: reduce is about 60% of an update. ([Reduce into the probe field](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#reduce-into-the-probe-field), 3d_world_space_implementations)
- Shade each pixel with eight hardware bilinear reads from probes: Eight threads per pixel, one bilinear read each; about 1.1 GiB of probes at 2^18. ([Shading and memory](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#shading-and-memory), 3d_world_space_implementations)
- Sample baked GI volumes per pixel, with no live pass: Nested baked volumes; trade-off: no baker, injection or validation exists in code. ([XRENGINE](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#xrengine), 3d_world_space_implementations)
- Get smooth per-pixel GI from coarse cascade 0 probes: Interpolate per-probe irradiance per pixel; trade-off is lost contact detail, restored by cascade −1. ([Final gather](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#final-gather), 3d_screen_space)
- Render stored cascades without tracing, at constant cost per pixel: Stored plane read per pixel; traced once, so it does not update. ([A radiance field stored on a plane](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#a-radiance-field-stored-on-a-plane), 3d_surface_probes)
- Shade each visible pixel from the nearest cascade 0 surfels: Composite gathers up to four cascade-0 surfels; one bounce only, tone-mapped with ACES. ([Composite (`composite.slang`)](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#composite-compositeslang), 3d_surface_probes)
- Shade objects anywhere in a lit scene from stored irradiance: Irradiance volumes: precomputed grid, static scenes only, no per-probe visibility. ([Irradiance volumes](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#irradiance-volumes), related_probe_gi)
- Light a static scene with baked probes in real time: Baked light-field probes: fast shading, but dynamic objects cast no indirect light. ([Light-field probes](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#light-field-probes), related_probe_gi)
- Give transparent surfaces indirect light without surfels: Volume clipmap of nested probe levels projected to SH, blended at level borders. ([GIBS and PICA PICA surfels](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#gibs-and-pica-pica-surfels), related_radiance_caches)
- Compute irradiance from records without octree lookups: Irradiance splatting draws records as quads, about 30 times faster than Radiance on two scenes. ([Splatting and time](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#splatting-and-time), related_radiance_caches)
- Interpolate indirect diffuse light from sparse records: Records with valid radii and weights; error tolerance sets spacing, and gradients reduce seams. ([Ward's rule](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#wards-rule), related_radiance_caches)
- Shade probes with six colours read by normal sign: Ambient cube; trade-off: six axis directions give coarse directional detail. ([Ambient cube](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#ambient-cube), supporting_techniques)
- Get directional ambient light from screen-space occlusion: Bent normal replaces the shading normal for ambient lookups; trade-off: one direction loses detail. ([Bent normals and bent cones](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#bent-normals-and-bent-cones), supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing)
- Get correct diffuse shading from directional radiance samples: Cosine-weighted gather over cascade 0; trade-off: centre-cosine weighting adds error. ([Lambertian BRDF](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#lambertian-brdf), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
- Interpolate probes smoothly across position and direction: Pentalinear blends probes and directions together; trade-off: 32 taps per lookup. ([Multilinear interpolation](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#multilinear-interpolation), supporting_techniques_transport_and_math)
