## G

- **G-buffer and deferred shading**: Per-pixel images of visible surface attributes written in one geometry pass. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: G-buffer and deferred shading](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#g-buffer-and-deferred-shading).
- **Gather**: Traces each interval against the scene BVH; visibility is 1 with no hit. [3d_surface_probes: Gathering (`gather.slang`)](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#gathering-gatherslang).
- **Gauss-Radau quadrature**: Gauss rule with one endpoint fixed as a node, exact to degree 2n−2. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Gauss-Radau quadrature](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#gauss-radau-quadrature).
- **Gear fix**: README workaround for cascade-seam halos, in place of the bilinear fix. [2d_implementations: Bevy](../topics/2d_implementations.md#bevy). Also in [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#first-public-implementations-and-the-shadertoy-lineage).
- **Generated surface atlas (Blackline Interactive)**: Atlas generated from meshes; probes sit on its texels, offset along the normal. [3d_surface_probes: A generated surface atlas (Blackline Interactive)](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#a-generated-surface-atlas-blackline-interactive), [single_frame_3d_rc: Surface probes](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#surface-probes).
- **Geodesic and tetrahedral subdivision**: Split faces into triangles, project vertices to sphere; classified by frequency and triangulation number. [supporting_techniques: Geodesic and tetrahedral subdivision](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#geodesic-and-tetrahedral-subdivision).
- **GGX**: Microfacet normal distribution with a long tail, usually with α = roughness². [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: GGX](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#ggx).
- **GI_BOOST scaling**: The resolve multiplies by a GI_BOOST of 3. [3d_screen_space_implementations: Alexander-Koutrakis/Alpha-Renderer](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#alexander-koutrakisalpha-renderer). Also in [3d_screen_space](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#final-gather).
- **Girard's theorem**: Spherical triangle area is α + β + γ − π. [supporting_techniques: Girard's theorem](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#girards-theorem). Also in [cascade_layout_and_merging](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#direction-maps-in-3d), [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#rc-for-ct-volumes-repository).
- **GPU distance fields from meshes**: Rasterizes each primitive's distance function into slices, culled by Voronoi bounds. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: GPU distance fields from meshes](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#gpu-distance-fields-from-meshes).
- **GPU hash tables**: Open addressing and cuckoo hashing for GPU key-value storage of sparse cells. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: GPU hash tables](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#gpu-hash-tables).
- **GPU voxelization**: Projects triangles along the dominant axis, rasterizes conservatively and averages into a 3D image. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Conservative rasterization](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#conservative-rasterization).
- **Gradient RC**: Stores a value and directional slope per probe direction; skips interpolation. [holographic_rc: Gradient RC](../topics/holographic_rc.md#gradient-rc).
- **Ground-truth pass**: One uniformly random direction per pixel per frame, accumulated with alpha. [3d_surface_probes: Ground-truth pass](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#ground-truth-pass).
- **GTAO**: Closed-form cosine-weighted visibility integral between slice horizon angles. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: GTAO](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#gtao), [godot_projects: Compositor effect projects](../topics/godot_projects.md#compositor-effect-projects). Also in [3d_screen_space_implementations](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#33ycdm-gtao-from-cascaded-horizons), [related_screen_space_gi](../topics/related_screen_space_gi.md#horizon-based-indirect-lighting).
- **GUPS**: Random 64-bit read bandwidth benchmark used to compare hardware. [3d_world_space: Parallel hash maps (cuCollections)](../topics/3d_world_space.md#parallel-hash-maps-cucollections). Also in [performance](../topics/performance.md#gpu-building-blocks).

## H

- **H-PLOC**: Binary build in one kernel from Morton ranges and PLOC merges, then collapsed wide. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: BVH](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#bvh).
- **Half HRC**: Drops every other probe row, removing the checkerboard for about 2x speed. [holographic_rc: Half HRC](../topics/holographic_rc.md#half-hrc), [holographic_rc_implementations: Half HRC](../topics/holographic_rc_implementations.md#half-hrc).
- **Halton sequence**: Radical inverse of n in the i-th prime base, digits mirrored across the radix point. [supporting_techniques: Halton sequence](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#halton-sequence).
- **Hammersley set**: First coordinate k/N; others are Halton radical inverses in the first primes. [supporting_techniques: Hammersley set](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#hammersley-set). Also in [godot_integration](../topics/godot_integration.md#screen-probes-in-122999), [related_engine_gi](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#brixelizer-gi).
- **Hankel functions**: Outgoing 2D Helmholtz Green's function, behaving like −ln r/(2π) near the source. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Hankel functions](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#hankel-functions).
- **Hardware ray tracing**: Replaces the screen-space marcher so probes see off-screen lights. [scene_representation_and_tracing: Hardware ray tracing](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#hardware-ray-tracing). Also in [3d_world_space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#why-world-space), [core_theory](../topics/core_theory.md#constant-cost).
- **Hash-based Owen scrambling**: Burley's form: bit-reverse, seeded Laine-Karras permutation, bit-reverse back. [supporting_techniques: Sobol sequence and Owen scrambling](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#sobol-sequence-and-owen-scrambling).
- **Hash grid (`shared/hash.slang`)**: Multi-level grid of surfel footprints keyed by cell position and level. [3d_surface_probes: Hash grid (`shared/hash.slang`)](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#hash-grid-sharedhashslang), [related_radiance_caches: SHaRC](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#sharc).
- **HBAO**: Marches screen directions to horizon angles and integrates the occluded hemisphere with falloff. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: HBAO](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#hbao), [godot_projects: Compositor effect projects](../topics/godot_projects.md#compositor-effect-projects). Also in [3d_screen_space](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#depth-buffer-marching), [3d_screen_space_implementations](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#33ycdm-gtao-from-cascaded-horizons), [related_screen_space_gi](../topics/related_screen_space_gi.md#line-sweep-ambient-obscurance).
- **HEALPix**: Equal-area iso-latitude pixels in 12·4^k cells, nesting ×4 per level. [supporting_techniques: HEALPix](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#healpix), [cascade_layout_and_merging: Direction maps in 3D](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#direction-maps-in-3d). Also in [3d_screen_space](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#directions-in-3d).
- **Hemisphere maps**: Hemisphere-only maps around a surface normal; neither octahedral nor equal-area. [cascade_layout_and_merging: Direction maps in 3D](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#direction-maps-in-3d). Also in [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#chebyshev-distance).
- **Henyey-Greenstein**: Phase function set by mean cosine g, isotropic at g = 0. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Phase functions](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#phase-functions).
- **Hi-Z and min/max depth pyramids**: Depth-buffer mip chain keeping min or max per 2×2 block, so rays skip empty regions. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Hi-Z and min/max depth pyramids](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#hi-z-and-minmax-depth-pyramids), [3d_screen_space_theses: Kaméus (2025): min/max probe placement](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#kaméus-2025-minmax-probe-placement), [scene_representation_and_tracing: Pyramids and atlases](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#pyramids-and-atlases). Also in [related_screen_space_gi](../topics/related_screen_space_gi.md#connections).
- **Hierarchical DDA**: DDA at each VDB tree level, stepping by node size to skip empty nodes. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Hierarchical DDA](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#hierarchical-dda), [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer: DexRT](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#dexrt), [godot_integration: HDDAGI](../topics/godot_integration.md#hddagi). Also in [3d_world_space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#single-frame-assessment), [related_engine_gi](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#hsgi).
- **Hierarchical discrete-ordinates scheme**: RC as discrete ordinates, with the ordinate count growing with distance. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Radiative transfer equation and discrete ordinates](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#radiative-transfer-equation-and-discrete-ordinates).
- **Hierarchical screen-space variance-based GI**: Horizon sectors over screen mips with a Chebyshev test on two depth moments. [related_screen_space_gi: Path of Exile 1's screen-space GI](../topics/related_screen_space_gi.md#path-of-exile-1s-screen-space-gi), [history_and_landscape: Path of Exile 1: shadows and GI](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#path-of-exile-1-shadows-and-gi).
- **Hit bits**: Visibility family member alongside erhe visibility rays and parent hit-distance tests. [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding: Connections](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding.md#connections). Also in [artifacts_and_fixes](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#connections).
- **Parent hit-distance test**: Binary merge test against stored parent hit distance, free given those distances; in a dense grid at the equal-error t0 it cannot act above the first hand-over, and measured it changes nothing. [single_frame_3d_rc: Leaks without history](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#leaks-without-history), [3d_world_space: Leak defences in world space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#leak-defences-in-world-space), [3d_surface_probes: Connections](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#connections). Also in [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding.md#connections).
- **Hit-point reprojection merge**: Reprojects coarser hit points into the finer probe; replaces radiance rather than compositing. [3d_screen_space: Merging across depth edges](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#merging-across-depth-edges), [3d_screen_space_implementations: lXByRh: hit-point reprojection](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#lxbyrh-hit-point-reprojection).
- **Holographic RC**: Suits 240p to 360p pixel art, or RC above output resolution. [holographic_rc: Holographic RC](../topics/holographic_rc.md#holographic-rc). Also in [2d_implementations](../topics/2d_implementations.md#setups-that-worked-in-2d), [3d_screen_space_theses](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#dellova-2025-performance-scaling-with-dxr), [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#storage-and-colour), [cascade_layout_and_merging](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#probe-placement), [performance](../topics/performance.md#what-the-numbers-show).
- **Hysteresis**: Exponential moving average x_t = h·x_{t−1} + (1 − h)·s_t. [supporting_techniques: Hysteresis](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#hysteresis), [3d_world_space_implementations: Trace](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#trace), [related_probe_gi: DDGI](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#ddgi), [history_and_landscape: Adoption: shipped, tried and turned down](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#adoption-shipped-tried-and-turned-down).

## I

- **Importance sampling**: Mean of f/p over samples from p; unbiased if p covers f's support. [supporting_techniques: Importance sampling](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#importance-sampling).
- **Index of dispersion**: Variance over mean, 1 for a Poisson process, above 1 for clustering. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Index of dispersion](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#index-of-dispersion).
- **Indirect dispatch**: Dispatch arguments written on the GPU, so no CPU round trip. [single_frame_3d_rc: Tracing and scene representation under `RenderingDevice`](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#tracing-and-scene-representation-under-renderingdevice), [godot_integration: Running the compute work](../topics/godot_integration.md#running-the-compute-work). Also in [related_radiance_caches](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#bevy-solari).
- **Inner parallax fix**: merge_fix mode 5 in Sohojoe's port. [2d_implementations: Godot](../topics/2d_implementations.md#godot). Also in [artifacts_and_fixes](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#straight-ray-fixes).
- **Interleaved gradient noise**: Pixel-coordinate formula with no texture fetch, designed for TAA. [supporting_techniques: Interleaved gradient noise](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#interleaved-gradient-noise).
- **Interval overlap**: Interval extended past the next cascade start by about √2 upper spacings; 0.75·√2 = 1.06 closes every gap on cell-centred grids; in 3D it lowers the merge leak only gradually and nearly doubles room error at 1.30. [cascade_layout_and_merging: Overlap](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#overlap), [artifacts_and_fixes: Cheaper fixes](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#cheaper-fixes), [artifacts_and_fixes: Gaps and seams between cascades](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#gaps-and-seams-between-cascades).
- **Interval transmittance**: Per-interval transmittance inside a cascade, so RC needs no moment test there. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Variance shadow maps and the Chebyshev bound](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#variance-shadow-maps-and-the-chebyshev-bound). Also in [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#beer-lambert-law).
- **IRLS**: Minimizes sum of |r|^p by repeated weighted least squares with residual-based weights. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: IRLS](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#irls).
- **Irradiance caching**: Computes indirect diffuse light at sparse records and interpolates between them. [related_radiance_caches: Irradiance caching](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#irradiance-caching), [godot_integration: Screen probes in #122999](../topics/godot_integration.md#screen-probes-in-122999), [related_engine_gi: Brixelizer GI](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#brixelizer-gi). Also in [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#radiance-caching-in-media-jarosz-et-al-2008).
- **Irradiance volumes**: Grid of irradiance over all directions, shaded by interpolation; baked, so no runtime updates. [related_probe_gi: Irradiance volumes](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#irradiance-volumes), [history_and_landscape: Non-English overviews](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#non-english-overviews). Also in [core_theory](../topics/core_theory.md#connections).

## J

- **Jacobi iteration**: Independent parallel updates from the previous iterate; converges when spectral radius is below 1. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Jacobi iteration](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#jacobi-iteration). Also in [light_transport_alternatives](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#multi-bounce).
- **Jenkins hashes**: Per byte adds and shifts, then a final avalanche of three shift-add steps. [supporting_techniques: Jenkins hashes](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#jenkins-hashes).
- **Jensen's inequality**: Averaged occupancy brightens through walls, the cause of thin-wall mip leaks. [artifacts_and_fixes: Connections](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#connections). Also in [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#marching-on-mips).
- **JFA+1**: Jump flooding with a final step-1 pass, fewer errors than plain JFA. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Jump flooding](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#jump-flooding).
- **JFA+2**: Jump flooding with extra steps 2 and 1 after the main passes. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Jump flooding](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#jump-flooding).
- **JFA²**: Jump flooding with log n extra passes. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Jump flooding](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#jump-flooding).
- **Joint bilateral upsampling**: Range weight computed on a full-resolution guide such as depth. [supporting_techniques: Bilateral filter](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#bilateral-filter).
- **Jump flooding**: Propagates seed coordinates in log2(N) passes with halving steps to build a distance field. [scene_representation_and_tracing: Jump flooding](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#jump-flooding), [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Jump flooding](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#jump-flooding), [holographic_rc_implementations: Youssef-Afella's Unity port](../topics/holographic_rc_implementations.md#youssef-afellas-unity-port), [2d_implementations: Scene representations](../topics/2d_implementations.md#scene-representations). Also in [2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders.md#gm-shaders-yaazarai-and-xor), [2d_articles_and_demos_jason_today](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_jason_today.md#part-1-naive-gi), [cascade_layout_and_merging](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#formats), [performance](../topics/performance.md#community-2d-timings), [godot_projects](../topics/godot_projects.md#phreakyx-voxel-cone-rc-as-a-gdextension), [godot_integration](../topics/godot_integration.md#sdfgi-the-shipped-gi), [3d_screen_space_theses](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#gavras-2025-spwi-on-metal).

## K

- **Kulla-Conty lobe**: Lobe built from directional albedo and its average, restoring rough-surface energy. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Multiple-scattering energy compensation](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#multiple-scattering-energy-compensation).

## L

- **Lambert cylindrical equal-area projection**: y = sin φ, with dy = cos φ dφ preserving area. [supporting_techniques: Lambert cylindrical equal-area projection](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#lambert-cylindrical-equal-area-projection).
- **Lambertian BRDF**: Diffuse BRDF f = ρ/π, so outgoing radiance is ρE/π. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Lambertian BRDF](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#lambertian-brdf).
- **Laplace diffusion**: Solves Laplace or diffusion equations by relaxation on a grid. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Laplace diffusion](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#laplace-diffusion).
- **Latitude-longitude map**: Texel (u,v) maps to θ and φ; solid angle per texel scales with sin θ. [supporting_techniques: Latitude-longitude map](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#latitude-longitude-map).
- **Lebedev quadrature**: Points in octahedral orbits, weights summing to 4π; smallest rule is six axis points. [supporting_techniques: Lebedev quadrature](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#lebedev-quadrature).
- **Light bleeding**: Overlapping occluders keep the bound high behind the nearer one. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Variance shadow maps and the Chebyshev bound](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#variance-shadow-maps-and-the-chebyshev-bound).
- **Light-bleeding reduction**: Maps p_max below a threshold to 0 and rescales the rest. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Summed-area variance shadow maps](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#summed-area-variance-shadow-maps).
- **Light defocusing**: Numerical angular blur of short-characteristics rays. [light_transport_alternatives: Sweep solvers for radiative transfer](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#sweep-solvers-for-radiative-transfer). Also in [artifacts_and_fixes](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#connections).
- **Light-field probes**: Baked probes storing octahedral radiance and distance; diffuse light from prefiltered irradiance. [related_probe_gi: Light-field probes](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#light-field-probes). Also in [performance](../topics/performance.md#related-techniques), [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#non-english-overviews).
- **Light leaks**: Failure mode: interpolation during merging leaks through walls, brightening shadows. [artifacts_and_fixes: Light leaks](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#light-leaks), [3d_world_space: Merge and interpolation](../topics/3d_world_space.md#merge-and-interpolation), [3d_world_space_implementations: Measurements against DDGI](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#measurements-against-ddgi), [3d_world_space_implementations: EggyStudio/3DEngine](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#eggystudio3dengine). Also in [2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders.md#gm-shaders-yaazarai-and-xor), [3d_surface_probes](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#artifacts-and-fixes-1), [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#simondevs-video), [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#limits-and-future-work), [holographic_rc](../topics/holographic_rc.md#what-it-fixes), [related_probe_gi](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#voxel-cone-tracing), [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#convex-offset-filtering).
- **Light on demand**: Hits mark a coarse dirty volume; only marked regions are relit next frame. [3d_world_space: Temporal reuse in world space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#temporal-reuse-in-world-space), [godot_integration: The SDFGI plan](../topics/godot_integration.md#the-sdfgi-plan).
- **Light propagation volume**: Injects reflective-shadow-map lights into SH grids and propagates them cell to cell each frame. [related_probe_gi: Light propagation volumes](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#light-propagation-volumes), [2d_shadertoys: Shaders that are not RC](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#shaders-that-are-not-rc). Also in [2d_implementations](../topics/2d_implementations.md#cpu-references-and-research-harnesses), [core_theory](../topics/core_theory.md#connections), [performance](../topics/performance.md#related-techniques).
- **Light trees and stochastic lightcuts**: Samples the light cut instead of fixing it, so correlated error becomes noise. [supporting_techniques: Light trees and stochastic lightcuts](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#light-trees-and-stochastic-lightcuts), [related_radiance_caches: GIBS and PICA PICA surfels](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#gibs-and-pica-pica-surfels), [light_transport_alternatives: Light sampling and guiding](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#light-sampling-and-guiding). Also in [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding.md#cascades-and-light-sampling), [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#implementation-and-bandwidth).
- **Line sweeping**: One march per direction serves every pixel it passes, O(1) per pixel. [light_transport_alternatives: Line sweeping](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#line-sweeping). Also in [performance](../topics/performance.md#related-techniques).
- **Linearly transformed cosines**: Clamped cosine under a fitted 3×3 matrix per view angle and roughness, closed-form over polygons. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Linearly transformed cosines](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#linearly-transformed-cosines), [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg: Sampling](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#sampling).
- **Low-discrepancy sequences**: Deterministic points spreading more evenly than random ones for quasi-Monte Carlo. [supporting_techniques: Low-discrepancy sequences](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#low-discrepancy-sequences).
- **LSAO**: Sweeps parallel lines over the depth buffer, keeping an occluder stack; O(K) per pixel. [related_screen_space_gi: Line-sweep ambient obscurance](../topics/related_screen_space_gi.md#line-sweep-ambient-obscurance), [light_transport_alternatives: Line sweeping](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#line-sweeping), [light_transport_alternatives: Berhard's sweep framework](../topics/light_transport_alternatives.md#berhards-sweep-framework).
- **Luminance and colour split**: Luminance at full resolution, colour at quarter; 4K in 8 ms instead of 12. [holographic_rc: Performance](../topics/holographic_rc.md#performance), [holographic_rc_implementations: jason.today/hrc](../topics/holographic_rc_implementations.md#jasontodayhrc).

## M

- **Memory by domain**: Cascade texel count per domain: flatland, 3D grid, or surface, with totals by ray factor. [core_theory: Memory by domain](../topics/core_theory.md#memory-by-domain).
- **MERGE_FIX**: Six-way merge switch: vanilla, bilinear, midpoint, Dolkar, parallax, inner parallax. [2d_shadertoys: The testbed line](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#the-testbed-line). Also in [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#first-public-implementations-and-the-shadertoy-lineage).
- **Merge operator**: Front-to-back alpha blend of adjacent intervals: radiance plus transparency times far. [core_theory: The merge operator](../topics/core_theory.md#the-merge-operator), [cascade_layout_and_merging: Merge kernels](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#merge-kernels), [holographic_rc: Geometry](../topics/holographic_rc.md#geometry), [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer: Integration and merging](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#integration-and-merging). Also in [3d_surface_probes](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#frame-order), [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#the-paper), [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#premultiplied-alpha-and-over).
- **mesh_surface_get_arrays**: CPU read of mesh triangles, since the engine exposes no mesh buffer RIDs. [godot_integration: World-space inputs](../topics/godot_integration.md#world-space-inputs). Also in [single_frame_3d_rc](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#tracing-and-scene-representation-under-renderingdevice).
- **Midpoint fix**: MfXyRj MERGE_FIX 2: shared leg to a midpoint, then one leg per upper probe. [artifacts_and_fixes: Cheaper fixes](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#cheaper-fixes). Also in [2d_shadertoys](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#the-testbed-line).
- **Min/max probes**: Two probes per tile at min and max depth, lerped by depth in one texture. [3d_screen_space: Min/max probes](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#minmax-probes), [3d_screen_space_theses: Gavras (2025): SPWI on Metal](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#gavras-2025-spwi-on-metal). Also in [cascade_layout_and_merging](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#connections), [performance](../topics/performance.md#3d-screen-space).
- **Mip bias under upscaling**: Negative bias log2(render/display) − 1, per AMD FSR guidance. [supporting_techniques: Mip bias under upscaling](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#mip-bias-under-upscaling).
- **modifierHack**: Rule p if B is below 16, else √B, scaling the community demo's intervals. [cascade_layout_and_merging: Intervals](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#intervals), [2d_articles_and_demos_jason_today: Part 2: building RC](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_jason_today.md#part-2-building-rc). Also in [2d_articles_and_demos](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#radiance-cascadescom-demo-and-osbornes-slide-demo), [artifacts_and_fixes](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#base-ray-counts-other-than-4).
- **Moment shadow maps and power moments**: Stores four power moments per texel and bounds shadowing from the moment problem. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Moment shadow maps and power moments](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#moment-shadow-maps-and-power-moments).
- **Morton order**: Interleaves integer coordinate bits so spatially close points get close keys. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Morton order](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#morton-order), [cascade_layout_and_merging: Position-first and direction-first](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#position-first-and-direction-first), [3d_world_space: Morton codes](../topics/3d_world_space.md#morton-codes).
- **MSE, RMSE, MAE, PSNR, MAPE**: Peak signal-to-noise ratio, 10·log10(MAX²/MSE), needs a stated MAX for HDR. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: MSE, RMSE, MAE, PSNR, MAPE](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#mse-rmse-mae-psnr-mape), [related_probe_gi: DDGI resampling](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#ddgi-resampling). Also in [2d_implementations](../topics/2d_implementations.md#unity-and-other-ports), [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#evaluation).
- **Multilinear interpolation**: Linear interpolation along each dimension in turn, from bilinear up to pentalinear. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Multilinear interpolation](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#multilinear-interpolation).
- **Multiple importance sampling**: Weights each sample across techniques with a balance or power heuristic. [supporting_techniques: Multiple importance sampling](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#multiple-importance-sampling). Also in [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#rcpg-product-path-guiding-with-radiance-cascades).
- **Multiple-scattering energy compensation**: Adds a lobe from directional albedo to recover energy lost by single-scatter microfacets. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Multiple-scattering energy compensation](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#multiple-scattering-energy-compensation).
- **Möller-Trumbore**: Ray-triangle test solving Cramer's system for barycentric coordinates and hit distance. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Möller-Trumbore](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#möller-trumbore).

## N

- **Nanite**: Unreal Engine 5 virtualized geometry: clustered LOD meshes streamed and shaded via a visibility buffer. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Nanite](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#nanite).
- **Neighbourhood clamping**: Clamps history to the current 3×3 neighbourhood's colour box, tighter in YCoCg. [supporting_techniques: Temporal anti-aliasing and reprojection](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#temporal-anti-aliasing-and-reprojection).
- **Next-event estimation**: Samples a light point, traces a shadow ray, converts area density to solid angle. [supporting_techniques: Next-event estimation](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#next-event-estimation). Also in [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#ray-casting-and-gathering), [holographic_rc](../topics/holographic_rc.md#3d).
- **Non-LTE radiative transfer**: Solves the radiation field with level populations off local thermodynamic equilibrium. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Non-LTE radiative transfer](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#non-lte-radiative-transfer).
- **Normal-offset sampling**: Samples at p + o·n so probes behind the surface are excluded. [cascade_layout_and_merging: Final gather](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#final-gather), [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer: RC for medical volumes (Rulkens)](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#rc-for-medical-volumes-rulkens), [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Shadow maps, acne and bias](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#shadow-maps-acne-and-bias).
- **Normalized throughput (derived)**: Rays per second: traced ray count divided by the pass time that traced them. [performance: Normalized throughput (derived)](../topics/performance.md#normalized-throughput-derived).
- **Nyquist-Shannon sampling**: Band-limited signals are rebuilt from samples above twice the band limit, else alias. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Nyquist-Shannon sampling](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#nyquist-shannon-sampling).

## O

- **Octahedral map**: Divide by L1 norm, fold the lower hemisphere outward over the diagonals. [supporting_techniques: Octahedral map](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#octahedral-map), [cascade_layout_and_merging: Direction maps in 3D](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#direction-maps-in-3d), [3d_world_space_implementations: Layout](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#layout), [3d_screen_space_theses: Gavras (2025): SPWI on Metal](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#gavras-2025-spwi-on-metal). Also in [3d_screen_space_implementations](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#three-rc), [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#mapping-and-culling), [related_probe_gi](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#light-field-probes), [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#non-english-overviews).
- **Octahedral solid-angle weighting**: Solid angle per texel is |p|⁻³; unweighted texel means are not sphere means. [supporting_techniques: Octahedral map](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#octahedral-map).
- **OIDN**: Apache-2.0 library of pretrained denoisers taking colour, albedo and normals. [supporting_techniques: OIDN](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#oidn).
- **On-surface caches**: Surface-bound 8x8 hemisphere radiance entries on texture-space islands. [3d_surface_probes: On-surface radiance caches (Tatzgern et al. 2024)](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#on-surface-radiance-caches-tatzgern-et-al-2024). Also in [single_frame_3d_rc](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#where-the-evidence-stops).
- **One-bit hit mask**: Binary visibility mask that costs about 50% more merge time. [single_frame_3d_rc: Leaks without history](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#leaks-without-history). Also in [3d_world_space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#single-frame-assessment).
- **Opacity correction**: Rescales sample opacity by step ratio so total absorption stays the same. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Opacity correction](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#opacity-correction), [2d_implementations: Scene representations](../topics/2d_implementations.md#scene-representations), [related_probe_gi: Voxel cone tracing](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#voxel-cone-tracing).
- **Opacity-gated cross blur**: 3x3 cross blur that skips neighbours of very different opacity, removing the checkerboard. [holographic_rc: Final gather and cleanup](../topics/holographic_rc.md#final-gather-and-cleanup). Also in [holographic_rc_implementations](../topics/holographic_rc_implementations.md#cute_framework-and-folkjs).
- **Opacity per sample**: Constant opacity per step makes results depend on step count. [2d_implementations: Scene representations](../topics/2d_implementations.md#scene-representations), [2d_articles_and_demos: Connections](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#connections), [2d_shadertoys: Media](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#media).
- **Open addressing with linear probing**: Places a colliding key in the next free slot after its hashed position. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: GPU hash tables](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#gpu-hash-tables).
- **Over operator**: Merges near and far intervals: near radiance plus near transmission times far radiance. [holographic_rc_implementations: Data and transport](../topics/holographic_rc_implementations.md#data-and-transport), [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Premultiplied alpha and "over"](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#premultiplied-alpha-and-over).
