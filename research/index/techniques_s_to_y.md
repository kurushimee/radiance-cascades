## S

- **SAH**: Split cost from child surface areas and counts; builders pick the cheapest split. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: BVH](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#bvh).
- **Samples per direction per frame (derived)**: Each direction cell gets A/16 samples per frame; one ray at 4×4-pixel probes. [3d_world_space: Samples per direction per frame (derived)](../topics/3d_world_space.md#samples-per-direction-per-frame-derived).
- **SAO**: Scalable ambient obscurance: Alchemy-style AO math reading a depth mip pyramid, cost flat with radius. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: SAO](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#sao), [godot_projects: Compositor effect projects](../topics/godot_projects.md#compositor-effect-projects).
- **Screen probes**: One probe per tile at the nearest valid pixel, optionally jittered by a Hammersley sequence. [godot_integration: Screen probes in #122999](../topics/godot_integration.md#screen-probes-in-122999), [related_engine_gi: Screen probes](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#screen-probes), [related_engine_gi: Screen probes](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#screen-probes-1), [performance: Budgets for single-frame 3D RC (derived)](../topics/performance.md#budgets-for-single-frame-3d-rc-derived). Also in [3d_world_space_implementations](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#eggystudio3dengine), [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#positions-on-3d-rc).
- **Screen probes with world intervals**: Screen-space probes whose rays trace through a world structure. [single_frame_3d_rc: Screen probes with world intervals (SPWI)](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#screen-probes-with-world-intervals-spwi), [3d_screen_space: Screen probes with world intervals](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#screen-probes-with-world-intervals), [3d_screen_space_theses: Gavras (2025): SPWI on Metal](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#gavras-2025-spwi-on-metal), [history_and_landscape: The paper](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#the-paper). Also in [3d_world_space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#why-world-space), [performance](../topics/performance.md#3d-screen-space).
- **Screen-space cascade −1**: Listed as a TODO in the code. [3d_screen_space_implementations: three-rc](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#three-rc). Also in [single_frame_3d_rc](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#sparse-world-probes-near-surfaces-split-rc), [3d_surface_probes](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#single-frame-assessment).
- **Screen-space indirect light (SSDO, SSIL, SSGI)**: Reads the previous frame's lit colour and blends it into ambient light. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Screen-space indirect light (SSDO, SSIL, SSGI)](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#screen-space-indirect-light-ssdo-ssil-ssgi). Also in [3d_screen_space_theses](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#gerassimov-2026-rc-against-unitys-gi), [performance](../topics/performance.md#3d-screen-space), [single_frame_3d_rc](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#where-existing-designs-lean-on-time), [godot_integration](../topics/godot_integration.md#frame-order-and-compositor-hooks).
- **Screen-space RC**: Probes and intervals in the depth buffer, marched with occlusion bitmasks. [single_frame_3d_rc: Screen-space RC](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#screen-space-rc). Also in [related_screen_space_gi](../topics/related_screen_space_gi.md#connections), [performance](../topics/performance.md#what-the-numbers-show), [godot_integration](../topics/godot_integration.md#single-frame-assessment).
- **Screen-space reflections**: Traces reflection rays through the depth buffer and reuses on-screen colour at hits. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Screen-space reflections](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#screen-space-reflections). Also in [related_screen_space_gi](../topics/related_screen_space_gi.md#horizon-based-indirect-lighting), [godot_integration](../topics/godot_integration.md#frame-order-and-compositor-hooks), [related_engine_gi](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#timings), [related_probe_gi](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#lambrus-comparison-of-engine-gi-classes).
- **SD-tree**: Spatial binary tree with directional quadtrees at its leaves. [supporting_techniques: Path guiding](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#path-guiding). Also in [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding.md#vlam), [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#cascades).
- **Seam length between cascades**: Mean gap 0.179 upper spacings (0.177 in 3D); overlap 0.75*sqrt(2) = 1.06 (0.75*sqrt(3) = 1.30) closes it. [artifacts_and_fixes: Gaps and seams between cascades](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#gaps-and-seams-between-cascades). Also in [cascade_layout_and_merging](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#overlap).
- **Sector casting**: Cones on one screen line form a sector; one sample updates every cone in it. [3d_screen_space: Depth-buffer marching](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#depth-buffer-marching). Also in [performance](../topics/performance.md#3d-screen-space).
- **Selective bilinear subsets**: Tries the 15 non-empty tap subsets and keeps the one whose world point is closest. [3d_screen_space_implementations: MXjcWK: selective bilinear subsets](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#mxjcwk-selective-bilinear-subsets).
- **Shader execution reordering**: NVIDIA's 2022 feature regrouping ray work across threads for coherence. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Shader execution reordering](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#shader-execution-reordering).
- **Shadow maps, acne and bias**: Light-space depth test; acne from shared texels, fixed by depth bias or normal offset. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Shadow maps, acne and bias](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#shadow-maps-acne-and-bias). Also in [holographic_rc](../topics/holographic_rc.md#3d).
- **Shared-exponent precision loss**: Small channels lose precision beside a bright channel sharing the exponent. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: RGB9E5](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#rgb9e5).
- **Short characteristics**: Solves transfer along a short segment to the nearest upstream cell boundary. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Short characteristics](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#short-characteristics).
- **Simplex grids**: Cut interpolation lookups from 4 to 3 in 2D, 8 to 4 in 3D. [cascade_layout_and_merging: Merge kernels](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#merge-kernels). Also in [single_frame_3d_rc](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#leaks-without-history), [artifacts_and_fixes](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#cost).
- **Sky aliasing**: Point-sampling a small sun in coarse top bins bands; integrate over each bin. [artifacts_and_fixes: Sky aliasing](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#sky-aliasing).
- **SKY_INTEGRAL**: Top-cascade sky switch; without dividing by 4 it runs four times too bright. [2d_shadertoys: The testbed line](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#the-testbed-line), [2d_shadertoys: Diagrams and helpers](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#diagrams-and-helpers). Also in [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#first-public-implementations-and-the-shadertoy-lineage).
- **Slab test**: Ray-box test comparing per-axis entry and exit intervals; zero components need no branches. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Slab test](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#slab-test).
- **SMAA and FXAA**: Detects edges, measures patterns with search and area lookup textures, blends neighbours. [supporting_techniques: SMAA and FXAA](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#smaa-and-fxaa).
- **Sobol sequence and Owen scrambling**: Base-2 digital sequence: XOR of direction numbers at the lowest zero bit. [supporting_techniques: Sobol sequence and Owen scrambling](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#sobol-sequence-and-owen-scrambling).
- **Software triangle BVH**: Triangle BVH traversal, rejected as too slow on mobile. [3d_surface_probes: Scene representation](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#scene-representation). Also in [scene_representation_and_tracing](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#options).
- **Sort-based binning**: Sort-based deposit binning, 1.5 ms for 10 M texels. [single_frame_3d_rc: Where the evidence stops](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#where-the-evidence-stops). Also in [3d_world_space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#ray-splitting).
- **Sparse RC**: Vanilla 3D RC storing only probes near visible surfaces, sized by camera distance. [3d_world_space: Sparse probes near surfaces](../topics/3d_world_space.md#sparse-probes-near-surfaces). Also in [holographic_rc](../topics/holographic_rc.md#3d), [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#volumetrics-in-3d).
- **Sparse voxel octrees**: Octrees storing only occupied octants, ray cast directly without dense grids. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Sparse voxel octrees](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#sparse-voxel-octrees), [related_probe_gi: Voxel cone tracing](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#voxel-cone-tracing). Also in [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#limits-and-future-work).
- **Sparse world probes near surfaces (Split RC)**: Split RC probes at a per-pixel level of detail, rays split into cascades. [single_frame_3d_rc: Sparse world probes near surfaces (Split RC)](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#sparse-world-probes-near-surfaces-split-rc).
- **Spatial reuse**: Two spatial passes at shrinking radii, 8 then 5 samples, shrinking near corners. [related_radiance_caches: kajiya](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#kajiya), [related_radiance_caches: Bevy Solari](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#bevy-solari). Also in [supporting_techniques](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#restir).
- **Specular cone**: One narrow cone per pixel whose aperture follows the specular exponent. [related_probe_gi: Voxel cone tracing](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#voxel-cone-tracing). Also in [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#split-sum-approximation).
- **Sphere tracing**: Steps by the stored distance, needs a minimum step and a hit tolerance. [scene_representation_and_tracing: Sphere tracing and cheaper fields](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#sphere-tracing-and-cheaper-fields), [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Sphere tracing](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#sphere-tracing), [2d_articles_and_demos: radiance-cascades.com demo and Osborne's slide demo](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#radiance-cascadescom-demo-and-osbornes-slide-demo), [2d_articles_and_demos: Otavio Peixoto, OPEngine](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#otavio-peixoto-opengine). Also in [2d_shadertoys](../topics/2d_shadertoys.md#fads-line), [2d_implementations](../topics/2d_implementations.md#scene-representations), [3d_world_space_implementations_grids](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations_grids.md#passes), [holographic_rc_implementations](../topics/holographic_rc_implementations.md#tfkyrw-youssef_afella), [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#integration-and-merging).
- **Spherical Fibonacci points**: Heights 1−(2k+1)/N and azimuths 2πk/Φ; near-uniform for any N. [supporting_techniques: Spherical Fibonacci points](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#spherical-fibonacci-points).
- **Spherical Gaussians**: Lobes a·exp(λ(μ·v−1)) with closed-form integrals and products. [supporting_techniques: Spherical Gaussians](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#spherical-gaussians).
- **Spherical harmonics**: Orthonormal sphere basis; bands 0–2 (9 coefficients) with clamped cosine give irradiance. [supporting_techniques: Spherical harmonics](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#spherical-harmonics), [related_probe_gi: Chen's Monter probes](../topics/related_probe_gi.md#chens-monter-probes). Also in [3d_surface_probes](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#on-surface-radiance-caches-tatzgern-et-al-2024), [cascade_layout_and_merging](../topics/cascade_layout_and_merging.md#direction-maps-in-3d), [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#radiance-caching-in-media-jarosz-et-al-2008).
- **Split deposit**: Hit in cascade k deposits transparency below k and radiance at k. [3d_world_space_implementations: Split deposit](../topics/3d_world_space_implementations.md#split-deposit).
- **Split-sum approximation**: Multiplies a prefiltered environment map by a BRDF scale-and-bias table. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Split-sum approximation](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#split-sum-approximation). Also in [core_theory](../topics/core_theory.md#specular).
- **Splitting direct light**: An emitter shaded directly keeps its geometry in the RC scene but not its emission, so nothing is counted twice and its bounce stays. [core_theory: Splitting direct light (derived)](../topics/core_theory.md#splitting-direct-light-derived). Also in [single_frame_3d_rc](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#where-the-evidence-stops).
- **sRGB**: Gamma curve: textures decode to linear before filtering, with alpha kept linear. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: sRGB](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#srgb), [2d_articles_and_demos: radiance-cascades.com demo and Osborne's slide demo](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#radiance-cascadescom-demo-and-osbornes-slide-demo). Also in [2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_gm_shaders.md#gm-shaders-yaazarai-and-xor).
- **SSAO**: Depth-only occlusion from samples in front of the surface, rotated per pixel and blurred. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: SSAO](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#ssao), [godot_projects: Compositor effect projects](../topics/godot_projects.md#compositor-effect-projects). Also in [single_frame_3d_rc](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#lighting-the-hits-and-the-composite), [godot_integration](../topics/godot_integration.md#frame-order-and-compositor-hooks), [related_radiance_caches](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#kajiya).
- **SSIM**: Structural similarity over local windows from means, variances and covariance. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: SSIM](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#ssim). Also in [3d_screen_space_theses](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#kaméus-2025-minmax-probe-placement), [rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg](../topics/rc_for_sampling_and_guiding_rcpg.md#evaluation).
- **Star discrepancy**: Worst mismatch between a box's point share and its volume; bounds error with Koksma-Hlawka. [supporting_techniques: Low-discrepancy sequences](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#low-discrepancy-sequences).
- **Stochastic SSR**: Samples GGX with Halton points, shares rays among neighbours, accumulates over frames. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Screen-space reflections](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#screen-space-reflections).
- **Stochastic texture filtering and stochastic interpolation**: Stochastic blending between cache entries hides blockiness; used by kajiya. [supporting_techniques: Stochastic texture filtering and stochastic interpolation](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#stochastic-texture-filtering-and-stochastic-interpolation), [related_radiance_caches: kajiya](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#kajiya). Also in [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#transmittance-estimation-peters-2025).
- **Subgroup operations**: Invocations share values in-register: basic, vote, arithmetic, ballot, shuffle and quad. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Subgroup operations](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#subgroup-operations).
- **Summed-area variance shadow maps**: Summed-area tables of depth and depth-squared give moments over any rectangle. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Summed-area variance shadow maps](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#summed-area-variance-shadow-maps).
- **Surface cache cards**: Offline per-mesh capture views that record material and cache lighting for ray hits. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: Surface cache cards](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#surface-cache-cards), [scene_representation_and_tracing: Lumen's surface cache](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#lumens-surface-cache).
- **Surface probes**: Surfels or UV charts carry the probes, with probes not placed inside walls. [single_frame_3d_rc: Surface probes](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#surface-probes).
- **Surfel RC**: Experimental surfel probes that avoid volumetric memory; raise stability questions. [history_and_landscape: Community site, wiki and tools](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#community-site-wiki-and-tools), [history_and_landscape: Adoption: shipped, tried and turned down](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#adoption-shipped-tried-and-turned-down). Also in [single_frame_3d_rc](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#where-existing-designs-lean-on-time).
- **Surfel recycling**: Marker decay plus neighbour counts recycle surfels; unread ones are forced out. [3d_surface_probes: Recycling (`recycle.slang`)](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#recycling-recycleslang), [related_radiance_caches: GIBS and PICA PICA surfels](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#gibs-and-pica-pica-surfels).
- **Surfel spawning**: Probe sets that converge over frames, ruled out for a single frame. [single_frame_3d_rc: What a single frame allows](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#what-a-single-frame-allows). Also in [3d_surface_probes](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#performance).
- **Surfels**: Small oriented discs spawned from the G-buffer, with no precomputation, meshes or UVs. [scene_representation_and_tracing: Surfels](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#surfels), [related_radiance_caches: GIBS and PICA PICA surfels](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#gibs-and-pica-pica-surfels), [single_frame_3d_rc: Surface probes](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#surface-probes). Also in [3d_world_space](../topics/3d_world_space.md#other-sparse-designs), [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#positions-on-3d-rc).
- **SVGF**: Motion-vector accumulation plus variance-steered à-trous filter, for one sample per pixel. [supporting_techniques: SVGF](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#svgf), [godot_integration: Screen probes in #122999](../topics/godot_integration.md#screen-probes-in-122999). Also in [3d_surface_probes](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#love-and-deepspace).

## T

- **Tangent-plane probe weights**: saturate(1 − planeDistance/(0.03·depth)), falling back to the nearest probe. [3d_screen_space: Final gather](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#final-gather). Also in [3d_screen_space_implementations](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#unreal-screen-space-bitmask-rc).
- **Temporal accumulation**: Moving average with keep factor up to 0.98, reprojected; off by default. [holographic_rc_implementations: pixi-GI](../topics/holographic_rc_implementations.md#pixi-gi), [2d_articles_and_demos_jason_today: Part 1: naive GI](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos_jason_today.md#part-1-naive-gi), [3d_screen_space: Temporal stability](../topics/3d_screen_space.md#temporal-stability), [3d_screen_space_implementations: Alexander-Koutrakis/Alpha-Renderer](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#alexander-koutrakisalpha-renderer). Also in [2d_articles_and_demos](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#single-frame-assessment), [3d_screen_space_theses](../topics/3d_screen_space_theses.md#kung-2025-rc-against-voxel-cone-tracing), [godot_projects](../topics/godot_projects.md#passes), [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#integration-and-merging).
- **Temporal anti-aliasing and reprojection**: Sub-pixel jitter per frame, blended into reprojected history. [supporting_techniques: Temporal anti-aliasing and reprojection](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#temporal-anti-aliasing-and-reprojection), [godot_integration: Volumetric fog](../topics/godot_integration.md#volumetric-fog), [related_radiance_caches: GI-1.0](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#gi-10). Also in [related_screen_space_gi](../topics/related_screen_space_gi.md#horizon-based-indirect-lighting), [history_and_landscape](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#exilecon-2023-and-path-of-exile-2).
- **Temporal filter**: Exponential average with weight 1/n, n clamped to 4 to 8, reading last frame directly. [3d_surface_probes: On-surface radiance caches (Tatzgern et al. 2024)](../topics/3d_surface_probes.md#on-surface-radiance-caches-tatzgern-et-al-2024), [related_engine_gi: Screen probes](../topics/related_engine_gi.md#screen-probes).
- **Temporal reuse**: Not required by RC theory; used for host limits, cost spreading and bounce feedback. [core_theory: Single-frame assessment](../topics/core_theory.md#single-frame-assessment), [related_radiance_caches: kajiya](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#kajiya), [related_radiance_caches: Bevy Solari](../topics/related_radiance_caches.md#bevy-solari), [supporting_techniques: ReSTIR](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#restir).
- **Temporal Super Resolution**: Jittered low-res rendering with motion-vector reprojection, disocclusion detection, history resurrection. [supporting_techniques: Temporal Super Resolution](../topics/supporting_techniques.md#temporal-super-resolution).
- **Tetrahedral (Kuhn) split**: Six tetrahedra per cubic cell; the bilinear fix then takes 2 or 3 upper probes per cell-centred lower probe, 2.75 on average. [artifacts_and_fixes: Cost](../topics/artifacts_and_fixes.md#cost). Also in [single_frame_3d_rc](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#where-the-evidence-stops).
- **Thin-wall leaks**: Walls thinner than one traced cell leak at every structure that cannot resolve them. [scene_representation_and_tracing: Robustness](../topics/scene_representation_and_tracing.md#robustness), [godot_projects: Limitations and discrepancies](../topics/godot_projects.md#limitations-and-discrepancies).
- **Time per megapixel (derived)**: Milliseconds of RC time per output megapixel, comparing designs across resolutions. [performance: Time per megapixel (derived)](../topics/performance.md#time-per-megapixel-derived).
- **Tone mapping**: Maps HDR radiance to the displayable range with curves such as Reinhard, ACES and AgX. [supporting_techniques_transport_and_math: Tone mapping](../topics/supporting_techniques_transport_and_math.md#tone-mapping).
- **Top-down merge**: Cascades trace in parallel, then merge one dispatch at a time from the top. [2d_articles_and_demos: Théo Le Goc (tlegoc), Vulkan](../topics/2d_articles_and_demos.md#théo-le-goc-tlegoc-vulkan), [history_and_landscape: Single-frame assessment](../topics/history_and_landscape.md#single-frame-assessment).
- **Triangular dither**: Triangular ±1/255 dither added to the 8-bit output. [holographic_rc_implementations: cute_framework and folkjs](../topics/holographic_rc_implementations.md#cute_framework-and-folkjs). Also in [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#storage-and-colour).
- **Trilinear fix**: Merge fix for leaks, applied where the budget allows. [single_frame_3d_rc: Where existing designs lean on time](../topics/single_frame_3d_rc.md#where-existing-designs-lean-on-time). Also in [volumetric_and_radiative_transfer](../topics/volumetric_and_radiative_transfer.md#dexrt).

## V

- **Variance-limited mipmapping**: Parent kept only if children's dispersion index is below 1.0; checked above optical depth 0.25. - **Variance trapezoid integration**: Averages 16 virtual depth rays against the variance-filtered depth mean + σ. [3d_screen_space_implementations: Unity: direction-first screen probes](../topics/3d_screen_space_implementations.md#unity-direction-first-screen-probes). - **VDB and GVDB**: Sparse wide tree of fixed-size blocks with near-constant-time random access. [supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing: VDB and GVDB](../topics/supporting_techniques_structures_and_tracing.md#vdb-and-gvdb).
- **Visibility bitmask**: Per-slice bit field of N_b sectors; constant thickness t; drives AO and one-bounce GI.
